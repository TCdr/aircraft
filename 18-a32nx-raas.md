## Summary of Changes

The Runway Awareness and Advisory System is an option of the A320's Honeywell EGPWS (A320 FCOM GEN aircraft configuration "RAAS"; used
as a line-up aid in PRO-NOR-SOP-11 TAKEOFF RUNWAY CONFIRM). This adds it to the A32NX, implemented from the Honeywell RAAS product
description 060-4564-001, section 4:

- **Callouts:** approaching runway on the ground and in the air (750 to 300 ft, with the Airbus 450-350 ft suppress window), on runway,
  on taxiway above 40 kt, extended holding on runway, distance remaining at landing and at a rejected takeoff, runway end, approaching
  short runway and insufficient runway length with their cautions, taxiway landing.
- **Runways:** from the sim's own airport database, so no airport map (Navigraph AMDB) is needed.
- **Priorities and power:** the calls yield to the EGPWS aurals and run while the EGPWC (AC BUS 1) is powered and the GPWS SYS
  pushbutton is not OFF.
- **Voice:** played word by word (25 WAV clips) through the predefined `custom_*_sound_NN` event slots of the Wave Data pipeline. The
  clips are generated by `tools/raas-voice/generate.py` with the Piper text-to-speech engine and its `en_US-ljspeech-medium` voice,
  trained on the public-domain LJ Speech dataset.
- **Setting:** flyPad Aircraft Options / Pin Programs, RAAS: Off, Feet (default) or Meters (the unit of the distance callouts).
- **OIT:** the A380X OIT flyPad gets the new pin program option too (off, as on the A380X EFB); without it the FBW_TYPECHECK build fails.
- 16 unit tests (`fbw-common/src/systems/shared/src/runway-awareness/Raas.spec.ts`).

## Cockpit API Changes

- New LVars `L:A32NX_RAAS_AUDIO_{word}` (Bool, true while the RAAS says that word), documented in `fbw-a32nx/docs/a320-simvars.md`
  (GPWS / TAWS).
- New flyPad setting `CONFIG_A32NX_RAAS` (`OFF` / `FEET` / `METERS`).

## Testing instructions

1. flyPad Aircraft Options / Pin Programs: RAAS Feet.
2. Taxi towards a runway: "approaching runway ..." at the holding point; line up: "on runway ...".
3. Taxi faster than 40 kt on a taxiway: the taxiway caution.
4. Land: the distance remaining callouts during the rollout (in feet, then with Meters in meters).
5. GPWS SYS pushbutton OFF, or RAAS Off: no callouts.
6. `npx vitest run fbw-common/src/systems/shared/src/runway-awareness/Raas.spec.ts` (16 tests).
