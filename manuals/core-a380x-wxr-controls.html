1. ND messages of the manual GAIN, ELEVN and TILT

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: ND, right side above the TERR box  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-02).

What it is for

The GAIN button and the ELEVN/TILT option list of the MFD SURV CONTROLS page kept their state but showed nothing. The ND now shows the manual radar settings as the A380 FCOM WXR MESSAGES (DSC-34-20-30-20 P 17).

What you see

SURV CONTROLS settingND message
GAIN AUTO, ELEVN/TILT AUTOWX in green (as before)
GAIN MAN, value 53GAIN 53% in cyan, above the mode line; with ELEVN/TILT at AUTO the mode line is empty and GAIN takes its place
ELEVN/TILT at TILT, value -12.0TILT -12.0° in cyan instead of WX
ELEVN/TILT at ELEVN, value 12500, QNH referenceELEVN 12500FT in cyan instead of WX
ELEVN/TILT at ELEVN, STD baro reference on that sideELEVN FL125 in cyan
Manual mode without a valueGAIN ---%, TILT ---.-°, ELEVN -----FT (or ELEVN FL---)
MODE MAPMAP in green (GAIN nn% above it with GAIN MAN); no ELEVN / TILT message
WXR OFFWXR OFF in white, no GAIN line

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
The radar picture does not change with GAIN, ELEVN or TILT.The MSFS radar has no gain and no height data; tilting its beam crashed the gauge.The settings show in the messages and on the VD line only.
Cyan is a choice.The A380 FCOM gives no colour; cyan is the colour of the manual modes of the same radar family in the A320 FCOM (DSC-34-SURV-30-30 P 10).-
The grey background the FCOM figure prints behind GAIN is not drawn.Not reproduced.-

How to use

  1. MFD SURV > CONTROLS, WXR block (WXR AUTO, MODE WX).
  2. GAIN: press the button to MAN, enter a value in the field below it (0 to 100 %).
  3. ELEVN/TILT: select ELEVN (enter an altitude or FL) or TILT (enter -15.0 to +15.0 degrees) in the field below the list.
  4. Read the message on the ND (WX overlay selected). Back to AUTO for WX.

2. GAIN, TILT and ELEVN take the FCOM defaults at the first selection

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: MFD SURV > CONTROLS, WXR block  |  Status: User-checked in the sim after a flight reload (2026-10-02): GAIN 50 %, TILT +3.0° on the ground, ELEVN 100 FT at CYUL.

What it is for

A380 FCOM DSC-34-20-30-20-10 (GAIN and ELEVN knobs): "The default GAIN value is 50 %", "The default ELEVN value is the current aircraft altitude", the default TILT value is +3.00° on ground and 0° in flight. The page applies a default only when nothing is entered, but a value nobody had written read 0, a valid entry: after a power-up the first GAIN MAN, TILT or ELEVN selection kept 0. The MFD now starts with the three settings at no entry.

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
First GAIN MAN after the MFD startsGAIN field 50 %; ND GAIN 50%.
First TILT selection on the groundTILT +3.0°; ND TILT +3.0°.
First TILT selection in flightTILT +0.0°.
First ELEVN selectionThe current altitude, rounded to 100 ft (FL with the STD reference).
Later selectionsThe value entered or set before is kept.

How to use

  1. Select GAIN MAN, ELEVN or TILT on the SURV CONTROLS page: the default value is entered.
  2. Overwrite it in the entry field if needed.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A380X on the ground, after a flight load or reload. MFD SURV > CONTROLS: GAIN to MAN.GAIN field 50 %; ND (WX overlay) GAIN 50%.
2ELEVN/TILT: select TILT.TILT field +3.0°; ND TILT +3.0°.
3Select ELEVN.ELEVN field = the airport elevation rounded to 100 ft (for example 100 FT at CYUL).
4Enter TILT -5, go to AUTO, then select TILT again.TILT -5.0° is kept (no default over an entry).
5Reload the flight, take off, and select TILT for the first time in flight.TILT +0.0°.

3. VD white line of the manual ELEVN and TILT

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: VD under the ND  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-03).

What it is for

A380 FCOM DSC-34-20-30-20: "The VD displays a white line indicating the elevation or tilt value". In manual ELEVN the VD shows a horizontal white line at the selected altitude; in manual TILT a straight white line from the aircraft symbol at the selected angle (zero tilt = the horizon as seen by the ADIRS), as in the FCOM VD figures.

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
ELEVN selectedHorizontal white line at the selected altitude, from the aircraft to the end of the VD range.
TILT selectedStraight white line from the nose of the aircraft symbol, up for a positive tilt, down for a negative one.
Selected altitude off the VD scaleNo line.
ELEVN/TILT AUTO, MODE MAP, WXR OFF or no WX overlayNo line (it goes with the ELEVN / TILT message of the ND).

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
The line shows only while that side's ND shows the WX function.Design choice: the line goes with the ND message.-
The tilt line is straight.Design choice, as in the FCOM figure: no earth curvature or beam refraction.-
The weather on the VD does not follow ELEVN or TILT.The MSFS radar has no height data.-

How to use

  1. Select the WX overlay on the EFIS panel and ND ARC or ROSE NAV (the pages with the VD).
  2. On MFD SURV > CONTROLS select ELEVN or TILT and enter the value.
  3. Read the white line on the VD.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A380X in flight, for example at 10 000 ft. CPT EFIS WX overlay, ND ARC 40 NM. SURV CONTROLS: ELEVN/TILT ELEVN, enter 12000.VD: a horizontal white line at 12 000 ft, ahead of the aircraft symbol.
2Select TILT, enter +3.VD: a straight white line from the nose of the aircraft symbol, rising to the end of the range.
3Enter -3.The line goes down from the aircraft symbol.
4Select ELEVN with a value far above the VD scale (for example 60000).No line.
5ELEVN/TILT back to AUTO.The line goes; ND shows WX.
6With TILT selected, deselect the WX overlay.The line goes with the ND message.
