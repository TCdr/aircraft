1. ATC MSG lights and RING inhibited in takeoff and landing

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: Glareshield ATC MSG pbs, ATC mailbox on the SD  |  Status: Not sim-tested on this branch; vitest specs pass (check-only OIT, MFD, SystemsHost, SDv2).

What it is for

During takeoff and landing the ATC MSG pb lights and the RING stay off. An uplink received then, or a sixth uplink that needs an answer while five already wait for one, is rejected: the ATC center gets an automatic ERROR downlink. A message that already waits flashes again after the phase.

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
An uplink waits, FWS phase 3 to 7 (takeoff power to 1500 ft) or 9 to 10 (800 ft to 80 kt)ATC MSG lights dark, no RING.
A new uplink arrives in those phasesIt is not shown; the center receives ERROR.
After the phase, a message still waitsThe ATC MSG lights flash again.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
Taxi is not inhibited.Design choice: the manual names taxiing in one sentence only; inhibiting the taxi would reject the taxi and departure clearances. Same phases as the COMPANY MSG memo inhibition.None.
A32NX unchanged.The rules are behind the A380X mailbox flag of the shared ATC datalink.None.

How to use

  1. Nothing to do: the inhibition follows the FWS flight phase.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1Hoppie connected, logged on: have the center send a message during the takeoff roll.No ATC MSG light, no RING; the center receives an ERROR message.
2Have a message sent before takeoff power and leave it unread through the takeoff.The lights stop at takeoff power and flash again above 1500 ft.

2. ARU start: STANDALONE and SYNCHRONIZATION NOT AVAIL

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: OIT FLT OPS side: Login page and FLT OPS STS page  |  Status: Not sim-tested on this branch; vitest specs pass (check-only OIT, MFD, SystemsHost, SDv2).

What it is for

The laptops connect to the FLT OPS network through the ARU, which operates 2 minutes after the FLT OPS ANSU is powered. A laptop started before that, or without the network, works as a stand-alone: STANDALONE on its Login page and SYNCHRONIZATION NOT AVAIL on the FLT OPS STS page.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
The ARU follows the FLT OPS ANSU.Design choice: the ARU supply and failures are not modelled.None.
A laptop started before the ARU stays a stand-alone.As the manual: restart it once the ARU operates.Log off and start the laptop again.

How to use

  1. After a power-up, wait 2 minutes before starting the laptops.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1Cold and dark, power up, start the FLT OPS laptop at once.Login page: STANDALONE.
2Wait 2 minutes, restart the laptop.No STANDALONE; FLT OPS STS without SYNCHRONIZATION NOT AVAIL.

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: Laptop keyboards (and the ACD keys with core/a380x-cockpit): OIS MENU, OIS XFR  |  Status: Seen working in the sim on develop with the ACD keys (2026-10-17). Not sim-tested on this branch; vitest specs pass (check-only OIT, MFD, SystemsHost, SDv2).

What it is for

OIS MENU shows the FLT OPS menu. OIS XFR starts X-OIS: the OIT shows the FLT OPS page of the other OIT with "X-OIS Activated", and follows it; OIS XFR again (or OIS MENU) ends it.

The ACD keys need core/a380x-cockpit.The ACD legends and click areas are there.Use the laptop keyboard keys.

How to use

  1. Press OIS XFR to see the other side's FLT OPS page; press it again to come back.

How to test

6. Printing a COMPANY COM message

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: NSS AVNCS > COMPANY COM > Inbox, pedestal printer  |  Status: Not sim-tested on this branch; vitest specs pass (check-only OIT, MFD, SystemsHost, SDv2).

What it is for

PRINT under a displayed Inbox message prints it (subject, sender, reception time, text) on the pedestal printer, through the FMS printer of the MFD. The AOC also gets a downlink inhibition (no messages sent, uplinks still received), which the NSS DATA TO AVNCS pb-sw drives (see "AOC hosted by the NSS AVNCS ANSUs" below).

7. VHF 3 failure stops the datalink; SCI 1 and SCI 2 failures

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Failures > ATA 23 Communication and ATA 46 Information Systems  |  Status: Not sim-tested on the core branch. Develop (986919394): unit tests only (A380xVhfProvider.spec.ts, AocAvailability.spec.ts), no sim test recorded.

What it is for

  • VHF 3 is the only data medium of the A380X datalink router (no HF or SATCOM datalink in the sim). A failed VHF 3 (flyPad failure VHF 3, id 23007) is now treated as not in DATA mode: CPDLC and AOC stop, as COM VHF 3 DATALINK FAULT tells the crew on the aircraft. Before, the datalink kept working with VHF 3 failed.
  • Two new flyPad failures: SCI 1 (NSS / avionics secure communication interface) and SCI 2 (NSS / avionics secure communication interface) (46008, 46009). With both lost the AOC is inoperative: it can no longer send or receive messages (A380 FCOM COM NSS/AVNCS DATA XFR FAULT). One SCI alone changes nothing.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
No ECAM alert: no COM VHF 3 DATALINK FAULT, no COM NSS/AVNCS DATA XFR FAULT.The FWS avionics network and ATA 46 COM alerts (and the ADS function failure 46010) extend develop alert code that is on no core branch; they stay on develop.Know which failure you set.

How to use

  1. flyPad > Failures > ATA 23 Communication: VHF 3 to lose the datalink.
  2. flyPad > Failures > ATA 46 Information Systems: SCI 1 and SCI 2 together to lose the AOC.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A380X powered, Hoppie connected, VHF 3 in DATA mode on the RMP. OIT COMPANY COM: request the METAR of the departure airport.The answer arrives in the Inbox.
2flyPad > Failures > ATA 23: VHF 3. Request the METAR again.No answer: the request fails. Deactivate the failure: requests work again.
3flyPad > Failures > ATA 46: SCI 1 only. Request the METAR.The answer arrives.
4Add SCI 2. Request the METAR.No answer: the AOC is off. Deactivate both: the AOC works again.

8. AOC hosted by the NSS AVNCS ANSUs; NSS DATA TO AVNCS OFF stops the AOC downlinks

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: NSS DATA TO AVNCS pb-sw and NSS MASTER sw (overhead NSS panel); flyPad > Failures > ATA 46  |  Status: Not sim-tested on the core branch. Develop (30af1a232): unit tests only (AocAvailability.spec.ts), no sim test recorded.

What it is for

  • The AOC ran while AC BUS 1 was powered. It now runs while one NSS AVNCS ANSU is healthy: "In the case of an ANSU 1 failure, ANSU 2 provides the AOC application" (A380 FCOM DSC-46-40-10-20). An ANSU is healthy when powered, not failed (flyPad NSS AVNCS ANSU 1 / NSS AVNCS ANSU 2), not reset (RESET panel NSS AVNCS) and with the NSS MASTER sw on; the OIT writes it (L:A32NX_NSS_ANSU_1(2)_IS_HEALTHY). With both SCIs lost the AOC stays off.
  • NSS DATA TO AVNCS pb-sw OFF (pressed in, OFF light) stops the AOC downlinks (messages and requests); the uplinks still come in (FCOM DSC-46-20-20-80). The systems host reads the pb-sw itself: 1 = normal, 0 = OFF.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
The AOC follows the ANSU health written by the OIT.The ANSUs are modelled in the OIT instrument; the systems host reads their L:vars twice a second.-
No COM NSS/AVNCS DATA XFR FAULT or AVIONICS NETWORK alert.Those FWS alerts are on develop only.-

How to use

  1. Keep the NSS MASTER sw on and the NSS DATA TO AVNCS pb-sw in its normal position for the company datalink.
  2. Press NSS DATA TO AVNCS (OFF) to stop sending to the ground while still receiving.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A380X powered, Hoppie connected, VHF 3 DATA. OIT COMPANY COM: request the METAR of the departure airport.The answer arrives in the Inbox.
2Press NSS DATA TO AVNCS (OFF light). Request the METAR again.Nothing is sent: no answer. Release the pb-sw: requests work again.
3flyPad > Failures > ATA 46: NSS AVNCS ANSU 1. Request the METAR.The answer arrives (ANSU 2 hosts the AOC).
4Add NSS AVNCS ANSU 2. Request the METAR.No answer: the AOC is off. Deactivate both.
