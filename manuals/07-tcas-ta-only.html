TCAS in TA ONLY: intruders shown as TAs

While resolution advisories are inhibited, an intruder that would be an RA is shown and announced as a traffic advisory; the TA ONLY message is centred on the A32NX ND as in the FCOM

1. What it is for

With every RA inhibited (TA ONLY selected, or below 1000 ft radio altitude), the TCAS kept classifying an intruder that met the RA criteria as an RA. The ND drew it as a red RA square, and since it was not a TA, there was no "TRAFFIC, TRAFFIC" and no TA state. A real TCAS in TA ONLY shows it as an amber TA with the TA aural. The classification is now capped at TA while all RAs are inhibited.
