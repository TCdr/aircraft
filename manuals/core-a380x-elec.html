1. ELEC alerts from the electrical network

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: E/WD; SD ELEC AC / DC and STATUS  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: AC BUS 2 / AC BUS 4 FAULT OK (2026-10-03), AC ESS BUS FAULT with STATUS and GEN OFF OK (2026-10-04). Other alerts not sim-tested (unit tests: 30).

What it is for

The A380X ECAM had the ELEC alert texts but nothing raised them. The flight warning system now computes them from the electrical network (busbars, engine and APU generators, TRs, pushbuttons), with the procedures, the flight phase inhibitions and the master lights of A380 FCOM PRO-ABN-ECAM-10-24:

  • ELEC AC BUS 1(2)(3)(4) FAULT, AC ESS BUS FAULT, AC ESS BUS ALTN (AC ESS supplied through AC 4: AC ESS FEED in ALTN), DC BUS 1(2) FAULT, DC ESS BUS FAULT, EMER CONFIG (all four AC busbars lost, master warning).
  • ELEC GEN 1(2)(3)(4) FAULT (stays, with its GEN OFF line ticked, after the generator is switched off, until it works again), GEN 1(2)(3)(4) OFF (GEN pb-sw OFF with its engine running, after 5 s), DRIVE 1(2)(3)(4) DISCONNECTED, APU GEN A(B) FAULT, TR 1(2)(ESS) FAULT (no master light, no chime), APU TR FAULT, BUS TIE OFF.
  • Procedure lines are sensed where the FCOM says so (switches set, OFF THEN ON resets, AIR DATA SWTG, WXR & TAWS / XPDR & TCAS SYS 2...). Lines that the FBW texts had from another FCOM standard and that the FCOM procedure does not contain are hidden.
  • ELEC AC BUS 2 FAULT shows LDG DIST: AFFECTED (FCOM: LDG DIST IMPACT ON WET/CONTAM RWY ONLY).
  • ELEC AC ESS BUS FAULT has its FCOM STATUS: LIMITATIONS (APPR & LDG) SLATS SLOW; INOP SYS all phases ADR 1, GPS 1, WXR 1, XPDR 1, TCAS 1, HF 1, FEED TK 2 MAIN PMP, STBY PITOT HEATG, L WINDSHIELD HEATG, DFDR; INOP SYS APPR & LDG SLAT SYS 1, CAT 2, GLS AUTOLAND, LS 1, TAWS 1; REDUND LOSS (STATUS MORE) RA SYS C, FEED TK 3 STBY PMP, TRIM TK L PMP, ENG 1+2+3+4 IGN A, PACK 1 CTL 1, PACK 2 CTL 1. GPWS 1, TERR SYS 1 and ADS-B RPTG 1 are not shown as separate alerts while it is active.

How to use

  1. When an ELEC alert shows, follow the E/WD procedure (the sensed lines tick by themselves as you act).
  2. Check the ELEC AC / DC SD page (called with the alert) and the STATUS page afterwards.

2. AC ESS and AC EMER loads

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: Overhead ELEC panel (AC ESS FEED); flyPad failures AC ESS / AC EMER  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-04) with the AC ESS and AC EMER failures.

What it is for

The A380X electrical model uses the A320 bus names: the real A380 AC ESS busbar (400XP) is the A320 "AC ESS SHED" bus in the code, and the AC EMER busbar (491XP) is the A320 "AC ESS" bus. About twenty consumers read the wrong one, so they were on AC EMER where the FCOM puts them on AC ESS. They now use the AC ESS busbar (TypeScript, cockpit XML and Rust):

  • SURV SYS 1 (XPDR 1, TCAS 1, TAWS 1, WXR 1), RA SYS C, the slat motor, the 427XP EHAs, the feed tank 2 main, feed tank 3 standby and trim tank left pumps, channel 1 of both pack controllers, the F/O OIT and the FLT OPS ANSU (A380 FCOM DSC-24, DSC-34-20-100, DSC-46-20-70, DSC-21).
  • The AC ESS FEED FAULT light shows a loss of the AC ESS busbar (FCOM DSC-24-20: "The AC ESS busbar is not supplied"). An AC EMER failure leaves AC ESS supplied: no FAULT light.
  • The manual crossbleed control is powered by DC ESS (FCOM DSC-36-30) and the outflow valve manual control by its controller supplies DC 1 / 2 or DC ESS (FCOM DSC-21-30-10). Before, both were on A320 buses that the A380 does not have, so they had no power.
  • With only the batteries (static inverter on AC EMER), the AC ESS loads (427XP EHAs, TAWS 1, XPDR 1...) are unpowered and the AC EMER loads (VOR 1, LS 1, GPS 1...) remain.

LimitationWhyWhat to do

  1. Nothing new to set. In an AC ESS loss, follow ELEC AC ESS BUS FAULT (AC ESS FEED ALTN).

3. XPDR 1 / XPDR 2, per-system TCAS faults and SURV XPDR alerts

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: Pedestal SURV panel; MFD SURV > STATUS & SWITCHING; E/WD; SD STATUS  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-04) with XPDR 1, XPDR 2 and both failed.

What it is for

The A380 has two surveillance systems, each with its transponder and TCAS: SURV SYS 1 on AC ESS and SURV SYS 2 on AC 4 (FCOM DSC-34-20-100). MSFS has one transponder: it now acts as the transponder of the selected system (XPDR & TCAS SYS 1 / SYS 2 on the SURV panel or the MFD SURV page). The XPDR 2 failure did nothing before.

  • XPDR 1 failed or unpowered: TCAS 1 is inoperative too; same for 2. The sim TCAS computer fault counts for the selected system only.
  • SURV TCAS 1(2) FAULT is per system: no false TCAS 2 FAULT when AC ESS is lost (TCAS 2 is on AC 4). They need the busbar of that system powered (ELEC AC ESS BUS FAULT lists XPDR 1 / TCAS 1 itself).
  • New SURV XPDR 1 FAULT, XPDR 2 FAULT and XPDR 1+2 FAULT (FCOM PRO-ABN-ECAM-10-34-20): caution, line XPDR & TCAS ..... SYS 2 (1) when the failed system is selected and the other one works; STATUS INOP SYS XPDR 1(2) and TCAS 1(2), plus AP/FD TCAS MODE when the failed system is selected; XPDR 1+2 FAULT: XPDR 1+2, TCAS 1+2. The TCAS FAULT alert of the same system is not shown with it.
  • MFD SURV STATUS & SWITCHING: XPDR 1(2) and TCAS 1(2) shown failed when that XPDR is failed or unpowered.

How to use

  1. Select the XPDR & TCAS system on the pedestal SURV panel or MFD SURV > STATUS & SWITCHING.
  2. On SURV XPDR 1(2) FAULT, select the other system as the E/WD line asks.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A380X on the ground, powered, XPDR & TCAS SYS 1 selected. flyPad > Failures > ATA 34 Navigation > XPDR 1.E/WD: SURV XPDR 1 FAULT with XPDR & TCAS ..... SYS 2. MFD SURV STATUS & SWITCHING: XPDR 1 and TCAS 1 failed. STATUS: INOP SYS XPDR 1, TCAS 1, AP/FD TCAS MODE. No separate TCAS 1 FAULT.
2Select SYS 2.The line ticks; AP/FD TCAS MODE leaves the STATUS; the transponder works again (as XPDR 2).
3Also fail XPDR 2.SURV XPDR 1+2 FAULT; STATUS: XPDR 1+2, TCAS 1+2.
4Clear both; with SYS 1 selected fail only XPDR 2.SURV XPDR 2 FAULT without the switching line; STATUS XPDR 2, TCAS 2, no AP/FD TCAS MODE.
5Clear it. flyPad > Failures > ATA 24 Electrical Power > AC ESS.No SURV TCAS 2 FAULT (before: a false one); XPDR 1 / TCAS 1 are listed by ELEC AC ESS BUS FAULT.

4. NSS AVNCS ANSU 2

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: OIT (NSS AVNCS applications)  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-04).

What it is for

A380 FCOM DSC-46-20-30: the avionics side of the network server system (NSS AVNCS) has two servers; ANSU 2 provides the functions when ANSU 1 fails. Only ANSU 1 existed (the ANSU 2 failure did nothing). ANSU 2 is now built and takes over; with both failed the NSS AVNCS applications (AOC, eLogbook, maintenance) are lost. Supplies (FCOM DSC-46-20-70): ANSU 1 AC 2 or BAT 2, ANSU 2 AC 1 or AC EMER or BAT 1, FLT OPS ANSU AC ESS or BAT 1.

How to use

  1. Nothing to set: the OIT avionics applications use whichever NSS AVNCS ANSU works.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A380X powered, OIT on an NSS AVNCS page. flyPad > Failures > ATA 46 Information Systems > NSS AVNCS ANSU 1.The OIT avionics applications stay available (ANSU 2).
2Also fail NSS AVNCS ANSU 2.The NSS AVNCS applications are lost on the OIT.
3Clear both failures.The applications become available again.
