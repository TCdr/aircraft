1. Interactive ND

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: Lateral ND, KCCU cursor; MFD DIRECT TO page (UTC)  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (by 2026-10-01).

What it is for

A380 FCOM DSC-31-20-30-90: with the KCCU cursor on the lateral ND in ARC, PLAN or ROSE-NAV mode, the flight crew revises the flight plan directly on the ND, without going to the MFD. The FBW ND was not interactive.

What you see

  • Interactive areas: a blue box around every waypoint, NAVAID or airport under the cursor. A click gives the list of the elements there (P.POS for the aircraft mock-up), then the revision list of the chosen one.
  • Revision list: the ident (green in the active flight plan, yellow in the temporary one), then: for a flight plan waypoint FROM P.POS DIR TO *, INSERT NEXT WPT, DELETE *; for a database element FROM P.POS DIR TO *; for an airport also DATA; for the aircraft mock-up DIR TO.
  • DIRECT TO page at the bottom of the ND: the DIRECT TO target field (with a list of the flight plan waypoints, or a KCCU entry), UTC and DIST, the options DIRECT, DIRECT WITH ABEAM, CRS IN, CRS OUT, and the CRS field next to CRS IN / CRS OUT (default course from the flight plan, typed NNN, with M or T for magnetic or true). CANCEL closes the page when there is no temporary flight plan.
  • INSERT DIR TO * / ERASE DIR TO * buttons either side of the aircraft while the cursor is in the ND; INSERT TMPY * / ERASE TMPY * for the other revisions.
  • INSERT NEXT WPT page: FROM and the revised waypoint (green, or yellow with IN TMPY), its latitude / longitude, and the NEXT WPT field. A click on a blank area of the ND creates a latitude/longitude waypoint.
  • DUPLICATE page (DIST(NM), LAT/LONG, RETURN, scroll arrows) when a typed ident is not unique, nearest first.
  • DATA on an airport opens the DATA / AIRPORT page on the MFD of the same side.
  • DIR TO UTC: the FMS has no predictions of the temporary flight plan, so the UTC at the target is estimated from the distance and the ground speed, on the ND and on the MFD DIRECT TO page. Below 30 kt ground speed it shows --:--.

How to use

  1. Set the ND to ARC, PLAN or ROSE-NAV (map, not the OANS) and move the KCCU cursor onto the ND.
  2. Click a waypoint, NAVAID or airport (or the aircraft symbol); pick it in the list if several are there.
  3. Choose the revision: FROM P.POS DIR TO *, INSERT NEXT WPT, DELETE * or DATA.
  4. Complete the page at the bottom of the ND (target, option, course, next waypoint) by clicks or KCCU entries.
  5. Click INSERT (DIR TO * or TMPY *) to activate, or ERASE to discard.
