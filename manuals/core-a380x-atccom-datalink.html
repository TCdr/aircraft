1. ATC COM pages and the SD mailbox

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: MFD ATC COM pages; SD MAILBOX  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: ATIS, REQUEST / OTHER REPORTS / EMERGENCY frames and the fixes of that test (frame delete, label layout, font sizes, ATIS time): OK (2026-09-27). Logon to BeyondATC (RJJJ) 2026-09-28, MAYDAY sent 2026-09-29, PAN PAN PAN sent 2026-10-02. Answering uplinks of a live controller (WILCO, UNABLE...) not recorded. Unit tests: 33.

What it is for

The A380 talks to ATC by datalink: the crew logs on to a center, sends requests and reports, receives clearances and answers them, and gets the airport ATIS as text. The FCOM describes the MFD ATC COM pages (DSC-46-10-20-30) and the SD MAILBOX (DSC-46-10-10-70). They are all built on their FCOM figures and connected to the FBW ATSU, the same datalink system as the A32NX DCDU.

NoteThe network is chosen in the flyPad: Settings > ATSU / AOC (Hoppie, BeyondATC or SayIntentions). The datalink needs VHF3 in DATA mode on the RMP.

Logging on: CONNECT

  1. CONNECT > NOTIFICATION: type the ATC CENTER (4 letters) and press NOTIFY. The page shows NOTIFYING, then the center in NOTIFIED TO CENTERS (with the time), or NOTIFICATION FAILED.
  2. When ATC accepts, CONNECTION STATUS shows the ACTIVE ATC (and the NEXT ATC after a transfer). DISCONNECT ends the connection.

Asking ATC: REQUEST

  1. Open the menus on the right (VERTICAL, LATERAL, SPEED, CLEARANCE, WHEN CAN WE EXPECT, OTHER) and pick the frames you need; up to 5 frames, each with its fields.
  2. Fill the fields (FCOM entry formats; amber boxes are mandatory). ADD TEXT adds free text. The bin deletes a frame.
  3. Press XFR TO MAILBOX: the request goes to the SD mailbox, where you SEND it.

Reports: REPORT &amp; MODIFY

The SD MAILBOX

Every message to and from ATC goes through the mailbox at the bottom of the SD. It shows the active ATC, the ADS status, the message and the answer buttons the message expects.

The message expectsButtons
WILCO / UNABLE (a clearance)WILCO, STANDBY, UNABLE
AFFIRM / NEGATIVE (a question)AFFIRM, STANDBY, NEGATIVE
ROGER (information)ROGER
A departure or oceanic clearanceACK / REFUSE

UNABLE and NEGATIVE can add a reason (DUE TO WEATHER, A/C PERFORMANCE, TURBULENCE) and free text; MODIFY opens the answer on the MFD. The message colours follow the FCOM status legend.

How to use

  1. Set the network in the flyPad (Settings > ATSU / AOC), VHF3 in DATA mode on the RMP, and your network callsign as FLT NBR on the MFD FMS INIT page.
  2. MFD system menu > ATC COM > CONNECT > NOTIFICATION: type the ATC CENTER and press NOTIFY.
  3. To ask ATC: REQUEST, pick the frames, fill them, XFR TO MAILBOX, then SEND in the SD mailbox.
  4. To answer ATC: use the buttons of the message in the SD mailbox (WILCO / UNABLE, AFFIRM / NEGATIVE, ROGER...).
  5. For the ATIS: ATIS page, airport of the request area, SEND REQUEST; tick AUTO UPDATE to get the new versions.
  6. In an emergency: EMER, MAYDAY or PANPAN frame, XFR TO MAILBOX, SEND.

2. Datalink connection with the flight number, and SEND FAILED

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: MFD FMS INIT (FLT NBR); FMS message area; SD MAILBOX  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: logon to BeyondATC after the FLT NBR fix OK (2026-09-28); flight number connection from the FMS and SEND FAILED without ATC OK (2026-10-02). FMS DATALINK NOT AVAIL not recorded as seen in the sim. Unit tests: 5.

What it is for

The INIT page FLT NBR field wrote the flight plan only: the field did not refresh and the entry never connected the datalink networks (FBW TELEX and the ACARS provider), so ATC logons and downlinks failed. And a message sent from the mailbox with no ATC center was dropped without a word. Now the FMS connects the networks whenever the flight number of the active flight plan changes, and the mailbox says SEND FAILED (A380 FCOM DSC-46-10-20-60).

What you see

The ATC COM pages send and receive through the aircraft datalink, which connects with the flight number of the active flight plan (FMS INIT, FLT NBR). It must be your callsign on the network (Hoppie, BeyondATC or SayIntentions: the one set in the flyPad, Settings > ATSU / AOC).

  • The flight number connects the datalink however it gets there: typed on the INIT page, from the company flight plan (SimBrief), from an activated secondary flight plan, or already there when you reload the flight.
  • If the connection is refused, or the datalink does not answer, the FMS message area shows FMS DATALINK NOT AVAIL (white). It goes away by itself once connected; typing the flight number again tries again.
  • A message sent from the mailbox with no ATC center connected (no logon done, or the center logged you off) shows SEND FAILED instead of nothing.
NoteWith BeyondATC: log on to the center shown in its Frequencies tab (e.g. RJJJ), above FL145. BeyondATC does not handle MAYDAY or PAN PAN by datalink: it answers NO MESSAGE HANDLING AVAILABLE FOR THIS REQUEST. Declare an emergency by voice with it.

How to use

  1. Enter the flight number on the MFD FMS INIT page (FLT NBR), or get it with the company flight plan. It must be your callsign on the network.
  2. If FMS DATALINK NOT AVAIL shows, check the ACARS provider, then type the flight number again to retry.
  3. Log on (ATC COM > CONNECT) before sending: without an active ATC the mailbox says SEND FAILED.

3. ACARS provider connection

Aircraft: A32NX and A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Settings > ATSU / AOC  |  Status: Late start and close / restart of BeyondATC seen working in the user's flights (2026-09-29); not re-checked after the final build. Unit tests: 4.

What it is for

The ATC and company datalink of the aircraft (CPDLC logon, requests, messages, ATIS and METAR) goes through an ACARS provider you choose on the flyPad: Hoppie, BeyondATC or SayIntentions. The aircraft must find it, then connect your flight number to it.

What happens now

  • Start the provider when you like. The aircraft looks for it every 5 s for 5 minutes, then once a minute, without end. Your provider, ATIS and METAR settings are never changed.
  • Flight number. Entered before the provider answers, it is connected as soon as it answers.
  • Provider closed. Within about a minute the status goes back to "Waiting for Connection"; start it again and the aircraft connects your flight number again by itself. With BeyondATC, log on to the ATC center again (it forgets the logon when it closes).
  • TELEX on. If FBW's TELEX service refuses your flight number, the ACARS provider is connected anyway (on the A380X, the FMS shows FMS DATALINK NOT AVAIL for the TELEX failure).

What to expect

  • No extra traffic with Hoppie: once your flight number is connected, the regular polls (every 45 to 75 s) check the provider.
  • Before this change, a provider started more than 5 minutes after the aircraft set the flyPad to NONE: select your provider again once if it shows NONE.

How to use

  1. flyPad > Settings > ATSU / AOC: choose the ACARS provider (Hoppie, BeyondATC or SayIntentions).
  2. Start the provider program whenever you like, before or after loading the aircraft.
  3. Enter the flight number (MFD INIT on the A380X, MCDU INIT A on the A32NX): it is connected as soon as the provider answers.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1Load the aircraft with BeyondATC NOT running. flyPad > Settings > ATSU / AOC: ACARS provider BeyondATC."Waiting for Connection".
2Wait more than 5 minutes, then start BeyondATC.Within about a minute the status turns to "Connected" (green). The provider, ATIS and METAR settings are unchanged (not reset to NONE).
3Enter the flight number before BeyondATC answers (restart the test with the flight number entered first).The flight number is connected as soon as BeyondATC answers.
4Close BeyondATC.Within about a minute the status goes back to "Waiting for Connection".
5Start BeyondATC again.The aircraft connects the flight number again by itself; log on to the ATC center again (BeyondATC forgets the logon when it closes).

4. SD mailbox fixes: FILE FULL after CANCEL, uplink @ markers

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: SD MAILBOX; MFD ATC COM MSG RECORD  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: FILE FULL no longer sticks after cancels, PAN PAN PAN sent to BeyondATC (2026-10-02); @ markers shown as cyan values (2026-10-03).

What it is for

Two mailbox problems found in the sim with BeyondATC:

  • FILE FULL for good. The mailbox keeps at most five open messages per direction and holds the next ones back with FILE FULL. A cancelled downlink (CANCEL of a prepared message) left the ATC function but kept its place in the mailbox file, so after five cancels the file stayed full: every new downlink, a PAN PAN PAN included, waited behind FILE FULL. A cancel now frees the place, the held messages move in, and FILE FULL goes when the file has room again (A380 FCOM DSC-46: "File no more full").
  • Triangles around values. Hoppie and BeyondATC mark the variable fields of a free text uplink with @ (CLRD TO @CYVR@ RWY @06R@), and the A380 display font draws @ as a triangle. The mailbox now shows the marked fields as main parameters, in blue (FCOM DSC-46-10-10-70), without the markers, as the A32NX DCDU does; an unclosed marker runs to the end of the text, and a word glued to a field ("@4611@.") stays glued. MSG RECORD drops the markers too.

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
Five downlinks prepared and cancelled, then a new oneThe new downlink is shown with SEND / CANCEL; no FILE FULL.
More than five open downlinksFILE FULL for the extra ones, until a place is freed (sent, closed or cancelled).
BeyondATC departure clearance "CLRD TO @CYVR@ RWY @06R@ ..."CLRD TO CYVR RWY 06R ..., the values in blue, no triangles.

How to use

  1. Nothing to set: CANCEL a prepared downlink as usual, read the uplinks as usual.

5. LOAD-SEC3: ATC route clearances and crossing constraints into SEC 3

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: SD MAILBOX LOAD-SEC3; MFD FMS SEC INDEX  |  Status: Route clearance tested in the sim by the user (self-sent uplink): LOAD OK, ATC F-PLN INSERTED IN SEC 3 (2026-10-02). Crossing constraints and the REJECTED ATC INFO page not sim-tested. Unit tests: route clearance, constraints, mailbox logic.

What it is for

A route clearance or a crossing constraint received by CPDLC can be loaded into the FMS instead of being typed: the FCOM puts it in SEC 3, as a copy of the active flight plan with the clearance in it, for the crew to check and activate (A380 FCOM DSC-22-FMS-10-40, DSC-46-10-40). The mailbox has the LOAD-SEC3 key in the 4th button position, on the uplinks that can be loaded only; it stays after WILCO, with CLOSE.

Uplink (CPDLC element)What is loaded into SEC 3
Route clearance: CLEARED TO ... VIA ... (UM79), CLEARED ... (UM80), AT ... CLEARED ... (UM83)SID (not once airborne), airways, waypoints, lat/long points, STAR and a new destination. The rest of the en route part is replaced; a UM79 route ends at its clearance limit.
Crossing constraints (UM46 to 53, 55, 56, 58 to 61): CROSS ... AT / AT OR ABOVE / AT OR BELOW / BETWEEN ..., AT ... speed, AT ... timeAn altitude constraint, a speed constraint (90 kt to VMO 340 kt, in knots) or an RTA on that waypoint of the flight plan. The RTA is kept per secondary flight plan and follows it on a swap or an activation; the VERT REV RTA panel now works on a SEC plan.

NoteThe constraint upload awaits in a plain statement, not as an operand: the sim's JavaScript engine can reject an await used as an operand and then blanks the whole MFD. No visible change.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
A duplicate waypoint ident is taken as the one nearest to the previous point.Design choice: no NON UNIQUE rejection.Check the route in SEC 3 before activating it.

Needs an uplink of one of these CPDLC elements.Live controllers rarely send them; BeyondATC route clearances were not seen.Send yourself the uplink (How to test).

How to use

  1. Receive the clearance in the SD mailbox and press LOAD-SEC3.
  2. Wait for LOAD OK (or LOAD PARTIAL), then read the FMS message.
  3. MFD FMS: SEC INDEX, SEC 3: check the flight plan (and REJECTED ATC INFO if shown).
  4. Answer the uplink (WILCO / UNABLE) and, if you accept the clearance, activate SEC 3 (or swap it).
