1. COMPANY MSG memo and voice gating, COMPANY MSG:PRNTR

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: E/WD memo area; overhead NSS DATA TO AVNCS pb-sw; flyPad > Failures > ATA 46  |  Status: Sim-tested by the user on develop on 2026-10-21 in the combined OIS batch 2 install (T7 NSS DATA TO AVNCS OFF, T8 METAR auto-print with the PRNTR memo 1 to 0, T12 both SCIs lost); not re-tested on this core branch.

What it is for

The A380 FCOM (DSC-46-20-20-80/85, DSC-46-40, ECAM memos) says the AOC raises its memos and voices through the secure communication interfaces (SCI), in the NSS to avionics direction. When that direction is cut, the crew gets no message indication. The FWS now follows it:

  • No COMPANY MSG memo and no COMPANY MESSAGE voice while the NSS DATA TO AVNCS pb-sw is OFF (alert COM NSS DATA TO AVNCS XFR FAULT) or both SCIs are lost (COM NSS/AVNCS DATA XFR FAULT). A message received during that time is not announced later.
  • COMPANY MSG:PRNTR replaces the COMPANY MSG memo while unread company messages (received weather reports: METAR, TAF, ATIS) wait for their automatic printout on the pedestal printer. The count is L:A32NX_COMPANY_MSG_PRNTR_COUNT, written by the AOC auto-print of core/a380x-ois; the memo goes back to COMPANY MSG, or off, once they are printed or read.

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
A company message arrives, link normalCOMPANY MSG memo and the COMPANY MESSAGE voice (the voice stays inhibited in the usual flight phases).
A weather report arrives and waits for its printoutCOMPANY MSG:PRNTR; it goes when the report is printed.
NSS DATA TO AVNCS OFF or both SCIs lostCOM NSS DATA TO AVNCS XFR FAULT or COM NSS/AVNCS DATA XFR FAULT; no COMPANY MSG memo, no voice for new messages.

How to use

  1. Nothing to set: the memo follows the NSS DATA TO AVNCS pb-sw and the SCIs.
  2. To read a message, open COMPANY COM on the OIT or the MFD AOC pages; the PRNTR memo goes when the printout is done.

How to test

Setup: A380X on the ground with the AOC working (core/a380x-ois and Hoppie network for weather requests), pedestal printer with paper.

#Do thisExpected result
1Request a METAR from COMPANY COM on the OIT (NSS AVNCS side).COMPANY MSG:PRNTR while the report waits, the report prints on the pedestal printer, then the memo changes to COMPANY MSG or goes out (PRNTR count 1 to 0).

3flyPad > Failures > ATA 46 > SCI 1 and SCI 2, then request a METAR.COM NSS/AVNCS DATA XFR FAULT; no COMPANY MSG memo, no voice. Deactivate both failures.
