The ram air turbine (RAT) of both aircraft can be stowed again after the landing, on the ground only, as the FCOMs describe; a request in flight or while an extension is still commanded is refused

1. RAT stow on the ground

Aircraft: A32NX and A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Ground > Services, row "RAT stow (maintenance)"  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user on develop: OK on both aircraft (2026-10-05). Rust tests in systems (ram_air_turbine_deployment.rs), a320_systems (hydraulic) and a380_systems (electrical); vitest ratStow.spec on core/efb-redesign.

What it is for

Once the RAT had extended (both AC buses lost, RAT MAN ON, A32NX EMER ELEC PWR MAN ON), nothing stowed it again: it stayed out for the rest of the session, and on the A32NX HYD RAT FAULT stayed on. On the real aircraft the ground crew stows it after the flight.

NoteA320 FCOM DSC-29-10-20 (a320_fcom.txt l.43637-43653): "The RAT deploys automatically if AC BUS 1 and AC BUS 2 are both lost. It can be deployed manually from the overhead panel. It can be stowed only when the aircraft is on the ground."
NoteA380 FCOM DSC-29-10 (a380_fcom.txt l.61250): "The green hydraulic system powers RAT retraction, on ground."

The systems now accept a maintenance stow request (L:A32NX_RAT_STOW_REQUEST) on the ground, while no extension is commanded. One shared Rust model (ram_air_turbine_deployment.rs) moves the strut of both aircraft, so the A320 and the A380X behave the same.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
The stow needs no hydraulic pressure.Design choice: the FCOMs say only "on the ground" (A320) and that green hydraulics power the retraction on ground (A380); the stow is a ground crew action, so it is not tied to the aircraft hydraulics being pressurised.-
The strut travels in 1 s.Design choice: the same speed as the stock extension; no FCOM value.-
No RAT on the A32NX model.The A32NX exterior model has no RAT yet: only the HYD page, HYD RAT FAULT and the RAT pump show the stow.-
A request in flight is dropped.Each request is evaluated once (written back to 0), so a request in flight does not stow the RAT after the landing.Request again on the ground.
The flyPad row needs the stationary aircraft.Design choice of the flyPad (like the other ground services); the systems themselves only need "on the ground".Stop the aircraft.

How to use

  1. After a flight with the RAT out, stop on the ground.
  2. Restore normal electrical power (engines or APU generators, or external power) so that no extension is commanded; on the A32NX the RAT MAN ON and EMER ELEC PWR MAN ON pbs are not pressed.
  3. flyPad > Ground > Services: press Stow on the row "RAT stow (maintenance)".
