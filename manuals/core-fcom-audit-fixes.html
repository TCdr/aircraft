Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: GPWS, OANS, SD, FADEC, FWS, ELEC panel, EWD  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user on develop: OK (2026-10-04). How to use

  1. Nothing to do: the systems behave as in the FCOM.
  2. TAWS FLAP MODE: MFD > SURV > CONTROLS, FLAP MODE OFF to inhibit TOO LOW FLAPS (e.g. a landing in a non-standard flap configuration).

How to test

#Do thisExpected result

10Reach F/CTL ALTN LAW outside ALT 1A (e.g. with flyPad flight control failures).The procedure shows MAX SPEED ..... 310 KT; in ALT 1A it does not.

2. A32NX FCOM fixes

Aircraft: A32NX  |  Where: E/WD, SD BLEED, CRUISE and WHEEL pages, TCAS  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user on develop: OK (2026-10-04). Unit tests: DeltaPIndication, AutoBrakeIndication, AllEnginesFailure, TcasGpwsInhibit specs; Rust test in pneumatic.rs (each fails without its fix).

The same audit on the A32NX found six one-line bugs. Each now follows the A320 FCOM.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
The APU START line does not say "(BELOW FL 250)".The 24-character ECAM line has no room; the condition gates the line instead, as for the other conditional lines.-
AUTO BRK never turns amber.The AUTO BRK failure that the FCOM links to the amber colour is not modelled.-

How to use

  1. Nothing to do: the alerts and pages behave as in the FCOM.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A32NX in cruise. ENG 1 BLEED OFF, ENG 2 BLEED ON, X BLEED closed. Set PACK 2 pb OFF.After 5 s: AIR PACK 2 OFF.
2In flight with WING ANTI ICE ON, at low engine power (cold bleed air); SD BLEED page.When a bleed supplies air below 150 °C, its bleed temperature is amber; with that ENG BLEED pb OFF (valve closed) it is not.
3In flight with a TCAS RA possible (traffic), trigger a GPWS alert (e.g. SINK RATE).The TCAS goes TA ONLY (no RA) while the GPWS alert is on.
4SD CRUISE page in cruise with delta P above 1.5 PSI.Delta P does not pulse. In the approach (flight phase 7) above 1.5 PSI it pulses, until it is below 1 PSI.
5On the ground, one engine running, AUTO BRK MAX armed; SD WHEEL page.AUTO BRK green (it was amber).
6In flight above 2 500 ft RA and below FL 250, APU off: all engines flame out (e.g. fuel cut-off).ENG ALL ENGINES FAILURE shows the APU ..... START line.

3. ADIRS fast alignment in 30 s

Aircraft: A32NX and A380X  |  Where: Overhead ADIRS panel  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user on develop: OK (2026-10-04). Rust tests in adirs.rs (the existing fast alignment test tightened to 30 s).

What it is for

A fast (re)alignment, the IR mode selector set to OFF and back to NAV within 5 s, took 180 s. Both FCOMs give about 30 s (A320 FCOM DSC-22_20-20-40, IRS alignment page: "a fast alignment takes 30 s"; A380 FCOM DSC-34-10-30-30). The shared systems code now takes 30 s.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
Only the fast alignment changed.A normal alignment from an unaligned IR is unchanged.-

How to use

  1. With the IRs aligned on the ground, set an IR mode selector to OFF and back to NAV within 5 s.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1Aircraft on the ground, IRs aligned (NAV). Set IR 1 to OFF and back to NAV within 5 s; start a stopwatch.IR 1 is aligned again after about 30 s (not 3 min).
2Repeat on the A380X.Same: about 30 s.
