1. Material 3 look of the Ground pages

Aircraft: A32NX and A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Ground  |  Status: Services and Pushback tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-01). Fuel, Payload and Taxi: approved by the user (2026-10-01), checked in the browser harness. Pushback map drag fix: not sim-tested.

What it is for

The flyPad pages get a Material 3 dark look on the flyPad cyan: tonal cards, dense list rows with switches and chips, a segmented control for the tabs. A shared Material kit (page, cards, section headers, list rows, status and action chips, switch, segmented control, buttons, progress bar, banner) on new theme tokens for the blue, dark and light themes is used by every redesigned page. The Ground pages came first. The functions of the pages do not change.

What you see

PageNew look
ServicesThree columns: the doors and the ground equipment as scrolling lists (switch or Request / Release chip, GSX status and fuel progress on the rows); the aircraft from above, nose up and zoomed on the fuselage, its doors coloured with their state, the holds and service tags (A380X two decks, A32NX one); the GSX card (switch, turnaround timeline of the departure or arrival, other GSX services as chips, GSX questions and messages). Status chips: GPU, tug, doors open, chocks and cones (A32NX).
PushbackThe map fills the left card. A control rail: system switch, Call / Release tug button, parking brake row, Backward / Paused / Forward, a steering dial over the direction slider, the speed slider, Left / Straight / Right and the controller switch. When the aircraft is at the SimBrief origin or destination, the Navigraph airport map (aprons, taxiways, runways, guidance lines, stands) is drawn under the pushback map, heading up.
FuelThe aircraft with its tanks on a wide card and a refuel rail (target, slider, start and stop, duration, plan summary). A380X: the whole aircraft outline with the tank quantities in tables (each feed tank now shows its own quantity; before, the four feed tanks of the drawing read feed tank 3). A32NX: the over-wing outline with its tanks filled, the quantities as tiles.
PayloadThe cabin and seat map on a full-width card with the holds under it (load over capacity, click to plan), then the load table, the boarding controls and the balance chart.
TaxiThe route form as cards, the runway crossings as banners, the map buttons and the frequency box in the kit.

Pushback map fix: the airport under the pushback map redrew only after the map centre had moved about 100 m, so the aircraft slid over a frozen airport while the map was dragged. It now redraws on every move.

  1. Open flyPad > Ground and pick the tab (Services, Fuel, Payload, Pushback, Taxi) at the top right.
  2. Use the switches, chips and buttons as before: open or close a door, request a service, refuel, board, push back.

4. Payload page: LOAD title row and balance chart

Aircraft: A32NX and A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Ground > Payload  |  Status: Checked in the browser harness on both aircraft; the user reviewed the first version in the sim (screenshot, 2026-10-03) and chose the grid end. Final version not sim-tested. Unit tests: chart fit.

What it is for

On the redesigned Payload page the LOAD title sat on its own line above the load table and the balance chart left an empty band in its card. Now:

  • The LOAD title sits on the PLANNED / CURRENT header row of the load table.
  • The SimBrief fill action is a full-width button under the table, as on the Fuel page. It shows with the same rule as before (an OFP loaded that differs from the planned load, no boarding or GSX payload sync running).
  • The balance chart fills the height of its card (never smaller than the former 280 px).
  • The weight grid ends at the lowest labelled weight (A32NX 40 t, A380X 270 t) instead of leaving an empty band under the envelope; the weight labels are centred on their grid lines, the unit sits under the lowest label, and the MTOW / MLDW / MZFW names are re-placed for the new scale.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
A full MSFS restart is needed after an update.The chart scale lives in the aircraft config files (flypad-payload.json5).Restart the sim after installing.

How to use

  1. Open flyPad > Ground > Payload; plan the load in the table or with the SimBrief button under it.
  2. Read the centre of gravity on the balance chart.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result

5. Independent captain and first officer flyPads

Aircraft: A32NX and A380X  |  Where: Captain and first officer tablets  |  Status: A32NX tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-01). A380X: no separate record; both tablets were used in the 2026-10-02 sim test of the redesigned pages (quick controls on both tablets OK). Unit tests: tablet index.

What it is for

Both tablets showed the same flyPad: their screens share one material in the cockpit model and one gauge drew on it, so one pilot changing a page changed it for the other. Now the first officer tablet has its own flyPad gauge (efb.html?Index=2), with a new material on its screen in the cockpit model.

What is per tablet and what is shared

Per tabletShared

The actions that change the shared aircraft state by themselves run on the captain flyPad only: the tug release after a pushback, the automatic loading of lighting presets, GSX remote control, and the pushback stop when the Pushback page is opened or left. A flyPad clears only the OANS taxi flags it has set itself.

6. Quick controls clicks no longer open the troubleshooting page

Aircraft: A32NX and A380X  |  Where: Status bar > quick controls  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-01).

What it is for

The quick controls pane is drawn inside the status bar. When the status bar shows an issue (red), a click on it opens the troubleshooting page, so every click in the quick controls, and the click that closes them, also opened that page. The pane and its backdrop now keep their clicks, and the backdrop closes the pane on the click itself (closing on mouse down let the click land on the status bar under it).

How to use

  1. Open the quick controls from the status bar, use them, close them with a click outside.
  2. To open the troubleshooting page, click the red status bar itself.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A flyPad with a red status bar (an issue listed in Settings > About > Troubleshooting). Open the quick controls.The quick controls open.
2Click a switch or slider in the quick controls.The control works; the page under it stays (no troubleshooting page).
3Click outside the quick controls.They close; the troubleshooting page does not open.
4Click the red status bar.The troubleshooting page opens, as before.

7. Checklists: always loaded, green check when marked complete

Aircraft: A32NX and A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Checklists  |  Status: Checked in the browser harness. Not sim-tested. Unit tests: checklist marks.

What it is for

  • Checklists sometimes missing. The checklist file was read without waiting for its text, so an empty list was returned; the flyPad stored (and froze) it and the checklists parsed later could not be added. The file is now read and parsed before the list is returned; a second call waits for the same read, and a failed read is tried again by the next call.
  • Green check. The list showed the done mark only for the checklists of the current or previous flight phases, so with the aircraft unpowered (flight phase 0) a completed checklist kept an empty circle. A checklist marked with Mark checklist as complete now shows a filled green check, whatever the flight phase. One with all items done but not marked keeps the empty circle.

How to use

  1. Open flyPad > Checklists, pick a checklist, check its items.
  2. Press Mark checklist as complete at the end.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1Load the aircraft several times (new flights) and open flyPad > Checklists each time.The checklists are always listed.
2Aircraft cold and dark (unpowered). Open a checklist, check all its items, but do not mark it.The list shows an empty circle for it.
3Press Mark checklist as complete.The list shows a filled green check for it, although the aircraft is unpowered.

9. Error details on the flyPad error screen

The flyPad error screen only said that something went wrong. It now shows the error (name and message), the first lines of its stack and the React components it was thrown in, so a crash can be reported and found.

  2. Press Reset Display on the error screen to recover.

10. Landing calculator above the wind limits, with a wind check

Aircraft: A32NX and A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Performance > Landing  |  Status: Checked in the browser harness on both aircraft (2026-10-03). Not sim-tested. Unit tests: wind ranking, calculators beyond the tailwind limit.

What it is for

A crosswind above the maximum asked "Calculate anyway?" and a tailwind above the limit gave an error and no result. Now the calculation always runs: the pilot sees the figures with a warning and can compare the other runways and the alternate. This is on this branch because it changes the redesigned performance widgets.

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
Crosswind above the maximumResults shown, with a red banner "Crosswind n kt, maximum m kt": above the maximum for this runway condition, figures for information.
Tailwind above the limitRed banner "Tailwind n kt, limit m kt". The distances, stop margin and MLW(PERF) continue the tailwind correction of the data and are marked extrapolated (~ and a dagger, amber), with a note under the results.
Wind check tab (beside Landing roll, red dot when the wind is above a limit)The destination runways ranked by wind: within limits first, most headwind on top; LDA, HEAD/TAIL, CROSS and LIMITS columns; each with a Use button that loads it.
Alternate card in the wind checkThe OFP alternate (ICAO editable), its METAR from the weather source of the settings and its best runway, with Calculate for <ICAO> <runway>.
Wind entered without a directionThe ranking asks for the wind with its direction (e.g. 330/45).

The limits used

A32NXA380X
Maximum crosswind, gust includedDry, good or wet 38 kt; 29, 25, 20 or 15 kt on contaminated runways (A320 FCOM PER-LDG-DIS-MAT)Dry / wet 40 kt; 20 kt slush or dry snow; 15 kt standing water or compacted snow; 5 kt icy
Tailwind limit15 kt10 kt

Gusts are included in the components as in FCOM LIM; a variable wind is taken from the worst direction.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
The wind check is not an Airbus function.It is a design choice of the flyPad: advice only, the flight crew decides.-
Figures beyond the tailwind limit are extrapolated.The Airbus data stop at the limit; the tailwind correction is continued.Use them as an indication only.
The alternate check needs an internet connection and the OFP.The METAR comes from the weather source set in the flyPad settings; the alternate from the SimBrief OFP.Enter the alternate ICAO by hand if the OFP has none.

How to use

  1. Fill the landing calculator as usual and press Calculate.
  2. If a red wind banner shows, open the Wind check tab beside the landing roll picture.
  3. Load a better runway with Use, or the alternate with Calculate for, and calculate again.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A380X, SimBrief OFP with an alternate loaded. flyPad > Performance > Landing. Enter a runway, a dry runway condition and a wind giving about 45 kt of crosswind. Calculate.Results shown; red banner "Crosswind 45 kt, maximum 40 kt"; red dot on the Wind check tab. No Yes/No prompt.
2Enter a wind giving a 15 kt tailwind. Calculate.Results shown with "Tailwind 15 kt, limit 10 kt"; distances marked ~ and a dagger in amber, and the extrapolation note.
3Open the Wind check tab.The destination runways ranked, those within limits first; LIMITS column says "Within limits" or which limit is exceeded.
4Press Use on the best runway.The runway is loaded into the calculator; calculate again: no banner if it is within limits.
5Look at the alternate card.The OFP alternate, "Fetching the METAR..." then its METAR and best runway; "Calculate for <ICAO> <runway>" loads it.
6Repeat on the A32NX with a 16 kt tailwind.Banner "Tailwind 16 kt, limit 15 kt", extrapolated figures.

11. RAT stow (maintenance) row on the Services page

Aircraft: A32NX and A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Ground > Services, equipment list  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user on develop: OK on both aircraft (2026-10-05). Unit test: ratStow.spec (row look and status text).

What it is for

The flyPad side of the RAT ground stow: a maintenance row that asks the systems to stow the RAT after the landing (A320 FCOM DSC-29-10-20: the RAT "can be stowed only when the aircraft is on the ground"). The systems side (L:A32NX_RAT_STOW_REQUEST, the shared Rust RAT model) is on core/rat-ground-stow; see that manual for the rules, the limitations and the full test.

CautionMerge both branches: without core/rat-ground-stow the row shows while the RAT is out but pressing Stow has no effect.

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
RAT stowedNo RAT row.
RAT out, aircraft stopped on the ground"RAT stow (maintenance)", status "RAT extended", button Stow.
RAT out, in flight or movingRow greyed, status "RAT extended · stow when stopped on the ground".
Stow pressedStatus "Stowing" for about 3 s; the row disappears when the systems have stowed the RAT, or stays if they refused (an extension still commanded).

How to use

  1. Stop on the ground with the RAT out and normal electrical power back.
  2. flyPad > Ground > Services: press Stow on the row "RAT stow (maintenance)".

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
