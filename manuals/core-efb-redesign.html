1. Material 3 look of the Ground pages

Aircraft: A32NX and A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Ground  |  Status: Services and Pushback tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-01). Fuel, Payload and Taxi: approved by the user (2026-10-01), checked in the browser harness. Pushback map drag fix: not sim-tested. Tug speed slider fix: confirmed in the sim by the user on develop (A380X, 2026-10-07).

What it is for

The flyPad pages get a Material 3 dark look on the flyPad cyan: tonal cards, dense list rows with switches and chips, a segmented control for the tabs. A shared Material kit (page, cards, section headers, list rows, status and action chips, switch, segmented control, buttons, progress bar, banner) on new theme tokens for the blue, dark and light themes is used by every redesigned page. The Ground pages came first. The functions of the pages do not change.

What you see

PageNew look
ServicesThree columns: the doors and the ground equipment as scrolling lists (switch or Request / Release chip, GSX status and fuel progress on the rows); the aircraft from above, nose up and zoomed on the fuselage, its doors coloured with their state, the holds and service tags (A380X two decks, A32NX one); the GSX card (switch, turnaround timeline of the departure or arrival with its Baggage step, other GSX services as chips, GSX questions and messages; see "GSX services link" and "GSX turnaround" below). Status chips: GPU, tug, doors open, chocks and cones (A32NX).
PushbackThe map fills the left card. A control rail: system switch, Call / Release tug button, parking brake row, Backward / Paused / Forward, a steering dial over the direction slider, the speed slider, Left / Straight / Right and the controller switch. When the aircraft is at the SimBrief origin or destination, the Navigraph airport map (aprons, taxiways, runways, guidance lines, stands) is drawn under the pushback map, heading up.
FuelThe aircraft with its tanks on a wide card and a refuel rail (target, slider, start and stop, duration, plan summary). A380X: the whole aircraft outline with the tank quantities in tables (each feed tank now shows its own quantity; before, the four feed tanks of the drawing read feed tank 3). A32NX: the over-wing outline with its tanks filled, the quantities as tiles.
PayloadThe cabin and seat map on a full-width card with the holds under it (load over capacity, click to plan), then the load table, the boarding controls and the balance chart.
TaxiThe route form as cards, the runway crossings as banners, the map buttons and the frequency box in the kit.

Pushback map fix: the airport under the pushback map redrew only after the map centre had moved about 100 m, so the aircraft slid over a frozen airport while the map was dragged. It now redraws on every move.

Tug speed slider fix: the handle of the vertical tug speed slider of the Pushback rail sat 5 px right of its rail (the slider library offset suits its own 14 px handle, the flyPad handle is 24 px). It is now centred on the rail.

7. Checklists: always loaded, green check when marked complete

Aircraft: A32NX and A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Checklists  |  Status: Checked in the browser harness. Not sim-tested. Unit tests: checklist marks.

What it is for

  • Checklists sometimes missing. The checklist file was read without waiting for its text, so an empty list was returned; the flyPad stored (and froze) it and the checklists parsed later could not be added. The file is now read and parsed before the list is returned; a second call waits for the same read, and a failed read is tried again by the next call.
  • Green check. The list showed the done mark only for the checklists of the current or previous flight phases, so with the aircraft unpowered (flight phase 0) a completed checklist kept an empty circle. A checklist marked with Mark checklist as complete now shows a filled green check, whatever the flight phase. One with all items done but not marked keeps the empty circle.

How to use

  1. Open flyPad > Checklists, pick a checklist, check its items.
  2. Press Mark checklist as complete at the end.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1Load the aircraft several times (new flights) and open flyPad > Checklists each time.The checklists are always listed.
2Aircraft cold and dark (unpowered). Open a checklist, check all its items, but do not mark it.The list shows an empty circle for it.
3Press Mark checklist as complete.The list shows a filled green check for it, although the aircraft is unpowered.

10. Landing calculator above the wind limits, with a wind check

Aircraft: A32NX and A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Performance > Landing  |  Status: Checked in the browser harness on both aircraft (2026-10-03). Not sim-tested. Unit tests: wind ranking, calculators beyond the tailwind limit.

What it is for

A crosswind above the maximum asked "Calculate anyway?" and a tailwind above the limit gave an error and no result. Now the calculation always runs: the pilot sees the figures with a warning and can compare the other runways and the alternate. This is on this branch because it changes the redesigned performance widgets.

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
Crosswind above the maximumResults shown, with a red banner "Crosswind n kt, maximum m kt": above the maximum for this runway condition, figures for information.
Tailwind above the limitRed banner "Tailwind n kt, limit m kt". The distances, stop margin and MLW(PERF) continue the tailwind correction of the data and are marked extrapolated (~ and a dagger, amber), with a note under the results.
Wind check tab (beside Landing roll, red dot when the wind is above a limit)The destination runways ranked by wind: within limits first, most headwind on top; LDA, HEAD/TAIL, CROSS and LIMITS columns; each with a Use button that loads it.
Alternate card in the wind checkThe OFP alternate (ICAO editable), its METAR from the weather source of the settings and its best runway, with Calculate for <ICAO> <runway>.
Wind entered without a directionThe ranking asks for the wind with its direction (e.g. 330/45).

The limits used

A32NXA380X
Maximum crosswind, gust includedDry, good or wet 38 kt; 29, 25, 20 or 15 kt on contaminated runways (A320 FCOM PER-LDG-DIS-MAT)Dry / wet 40 kt; 20 kt slush or dry snow; 15 kt standing water or compacted snow; 5 kt icy
Tailwind limit15 kt10 kt

Gusts are included in the components as in FCOM LIM; a variable wind is taken from the worst direction.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
The wind check is not an Airbus function.It is a design choice of the flyPad: advice only, the flight crew decides.-
Figures beyond the tailwind limit are extrapolated.The Airbus data stop at the limit; the tailwind correction is continued.Use them as an indication only.
The alternate check needs an internet connection and the OFP.The METAR comes from the weather source set in the flyPad settings; the alternate from the SimBrief OFP.Enter the alternate ICAO by hand if the OFP has none.

How to use

  1. Fill the landing calculator as usual and press Calculate.
  2. If a red wind banner shows, open the Wind check tab beside the landing roll picture.
  3. Load a better runway with Use, or the alternate with Calculate for, and calculate again.

How to test

2Enter a wind giving a 15 kt tailwind. Calculate.Results shown with "Tailwind 15 kt, limit 10 kt"; distances marked ~ and a dagger in amber, and the extrapolation note.

What it is for

The flyPad had three fixed themes: Blue, Dark and Light. A fourth choice, Custom, lets the pilot pick the colours: a base (the surfaces and text of the Gray, Black or Light theme) plus a primary and a secondary colour. The Blue, Dark and Light themes keep their exact colours.

  • Primary colour: buttons, switches, progress bars, chart lines.
  • Secondary colour: selected tabs, segmented buttons, chips and the navigation rail. "Same" uses the primary colour.
  • The flyPad adjusts only the lightness of the picked colours until every text and control pair reaches the WCAG 2.1 AA contrast (4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for controls). A contrast list beside a preview card shows each pair with "AA" (or "FAIL").
  • Amber and red are the alert colours: the preset discs offer none, and a colour close to their hues (picked on the wheel or typed) is accepted with a note that buttons and switches in it may look like warnings.
  • The pencil button at the end of the colour discs opens the colour wheel: the 12 pure colours of the painter’s wheel (yellow at the top, then clockwise yellow-green, green, sky blue, blue, violet, magenta, crimson, red, red-orange, orange, amber) on 5 rings, from the pure colour at the rim to pale tints next to the centre. Every wedge can be picked, red and yellow included; a theme stored in red or yellow is kept. Beside the wheel: the picked colour, a hex field (type a code, then Enter), a preview, the contrast checks of that colour, and, when the flyPad must adjust the colour for the chosen base, the shade it will really use. Nothing changes before Apply; Cancel keeps the theme as it was. The secondary wheel also offers "Same"; only the hue and colourfulness of a secondary colour count (the base sets its lightness).
  • The theme applies to both tablets and to the Payload balance chart. An existing user keeps their theme after the update (the old setting is carried over once).

Light theme fix: several titles and labels were hard-coded white and disappeared on the Light theme (and on a Light base): the Settings page title, the ATC frequency cards and callsign, the checklist and failure (ATA) titles, the preset names, the aircraft symbol of the pushback map, the battery level and the slider labels. They now use the theme text colour; a selected ATC frequency card uses its own text colour.

How to use

  1. Open flyPad > Settings > flyPad and scroll to Theme.
  2. Press Blue, Dark or Light for a standard theme, or Custom.
  3. Custom: pick the Base (Gray, Black, Light), then a Primary color and a Secondary color disc, or press the pencil button to pick it on the colour wheel (or type a hex code, then Enter), then Apply.
  4. Check the contrast list beside the preview card; press Reset to Blue to go back to the usual cyan flyPad.

13. Performance &gt; Buffet: A32NX buffet onset envelope (coffin corner)

Aircraft: A32NX  |  Where: flyPad > Performance > Buffet  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user on develop: Live page in cruise and in a turn OK, REC MAX 398 as on the MCDU (2026-10-06); What if checked from a user screenshot (2026-10-07); Live / What if pill row fix OK (2026-10-07). Unit tests: 41 (buffet, buffetLive, buffetUnits, RecMaxFlightLevel).

What it is for

A new Performance tab, on the A32NX only: the buffet onset envelope (the coffin corner) from the A320 FCOM LIM-13 BUFFET ONSET chart, for the aircraft now (Live) or for any weight, CG, level, Mach and bank (What if). It shows how much margin is left before the low speed and high speed buffet, how far the aircraft can bank, the 1.3 g ceiling and the FMS REC MAX.

  • The digitised FCOM chart (Mach, pressure altitude, CG, weight) is reduced to one lift table scaled by the ISA pressure ratio, with the chart CG correction (within 1.5 t of the 18 curves; within about 1 % of the QRH M .78 1.3 / 1.4 g lines).
  • REC MAX: the FMS value of the MCDU PROG page (A320 FCOM DSC-22_20-40-30: lowest of the 0.3 g buffet margin, MAX CRZ level flight, 300 ft/min at MAX CLB, green dot to VMO/MMO, the certified FL 398). The MCDU now publishes it in L:A32NX_FM_REC_MAX_FL (0 without a gross weight) for the flyPad.
  • The A380X has no Buffet tab: the A380 FCOM has no buffet onset chart.

What it is for

15. GSX turnaround: completed services remembered, Baggage steps

Aircraft: A32NX and A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Ground > Services, GSX services card > Turnaround  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user on develop (A380X, 2026-10-07): Done kept after GSX reset the boarding to available (recording), Baggage row Done (recording: boarding cargo 50 to 100 %), Turnaround horizontal scrollbar gone. Unit tests: gsxTurnaround, GsxServicesPanel.

What it is for

Needs the third-party add-on GSX Pro. GSX gives one completion signal, the service state 6 "completed", and does not keep it: in the sim (A32NX, 2026-10-06) GSX set the boarding state back to 1 (available) 14 s after the end of the boarding, so the turnaround offered the boarding again as if it had not been done.

  • Completed services remembered. The flyPad itself remembers the completed boarding, deboarding, catering and refuelling of the current turnaround (also across flyPad page changes). A service requested or running again is forgotten; the boarding is forgotten when a deboarding starts, and the reverse; everything is forgotten at the end of the turnaround (an engine running or the pushback requested). The jet bridge, stairs and GPU follow the sim.
  • Baggage steps. A Baggage row after Board in the departure turnaround (catering, boarding, baggage, pushback) and after Deboard in the arrival turnaround (deboarding, baggage, catering). GSX has no baggage service to request: it loads the baggage during the boarding and unloads it during the deboarding, so the row has no Request button ("Loaded with the boarding" / "Unloaded with the deboarding" while idle) and shows the GSX baggage progress (L:FSDT_GSX_BOARDING_CARGO_PERCENT / L:FSDT_GSX_DEBOARDING_CARGO_PERCENT). It is Done when 100 % is reached after the loading was seen running in this turnaround (a 100 % left from an earlier loading does not count), remembered and forgotten with its boarding or deboarding.
  • List fixes. The Turnaround list scrolls vertically only (no horizontal scrollbar), and the progress bar of a step sits in its own full-width box under the texts (it was pushed out of the card).
