1. Accumulator start pressure above the pump threshold

Aircraft: A32NX and A380X  |  Where: Hydraulic model, no control  |  Status: Not sim-tested. One unit test; the Rust test suites passed.

What it is for

An electro-hydrostatic actuator (EHA) has a small accumulator that must hold more than 100 psi for its electric pump to run. At every start the sim draws the accumulator pressure at random (mean 800 psi, spread 200 psi) and the draw was cut only at 0 psi. About one start in 4300 therefore gave an accumulator below the pump threshold: a spoiler EHA then could not move on its electric backup, and the unit tests that move an EHA failed at random.

The start pressure is now cut at three spreads below the mean (200 psi), as a serviced accumulator is never charged lower than that; a flat accumulator is a failure, not a random start state. The pump threshold of 100 psi got a name in the code.

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
Any start of the sim, normal draw (almost always)No change: the accumulator starts between 200 psi and its maximum, about 800 psi.
The rare very low draw (about 1 in 4300 starts)Before: the accumulator started under 100 psi and the spoiler EHA could not move on its electric pump. Now: it starts at 200 psi and the EHA moves.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
A failure that empties the accumulator is not covered.The fix only changes the random start state.-
