A380X cargo doors follow the hydraulics

The A380X forward and aft cargo doors stay locked when the ground crew is asked to open them while an engine runs (their electric pumps are inhibited), the request is carried out once the engines are stopped, and a moving door holds its position; the cargo doors of both aircraft open fully and are held locked open

1. Cargo doors locked while their pumps are inhibited

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: Systems; flyPad > Ground > Services > Doors, row "Cargo fwd + aft" (core/efb-redesign)  |  Status: Sim-tested by the user, recorder-checked: PASS (the close after a normal open was not run). Rust tests: 4 in a380_systems (inhibited with an engine running; stays locked when opened with an engine running; opens once the engines are stopped; holds its position when an engine starts while it moves).

What it is for

On the A380X the cargo doors are moved by the green electric pumps. When the ground crew was asked to open a cargo door while an engine ran, the door unlocked anyway (0.24 % open in a sim run) although the pumps that move it are inhibited then. The cargo doors now follow the hydraulics.

NoteA380 FCOM DSC-29-10 P 3: the green electric pumps are the only power of the cargo door actuation, and they are inhibited as soon as an engine runs. DSC-52-30-40-10: an inhibited door is isolated from the hydraulics, there is no power to open it.

The systems now compute whether the cargo door operation is inhibited. It is inhibited as soon as any of the four engines runs; this is the same rule as the one that stops the green electric pumps in the A380X hydraulics (a placeholder based on the engine oil pressure, which covers the FCOM cases: engines 1 and 2 running, engines 3 and 4 running, taxi and flight). The state is published as L:A32NX_HYD_CARGO_DOOR_OPERATION_INHIBITED (1 = inhibited).

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
Engine running, a closed cargo door is asked to openThe door stays closed and locked. The request is kept.
Engines stopped, the request still pending (or asked again)The door unlocks and opens (about 50 s); both doors, forward and aft.
Door moving or open, an engine startsThe door holds its position where it is (no further motion). It is not closed, so the usual open cargo door alerts apply (DOOR FWD CARGO / AFT CARGO NOT CLOSED, see the test).
Door held open, the request is switched off, then the engines are stoppedThe inhibition ends, and the door closes and locks about 35 s later.
Engines stopped, normal open and closeAs before: the doors open, then close and lock.
flyPad, engine running, door closedRow "Cargo fwd + aft": "Closed · no hydraulic power, engine running"; the switch is greyed out (needs core/efb-redesign, see the next feature).

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
The inhibition is "any engine running".The A380X electric pump controllers use a placeholder (the oil pressure of the engines) for the same condition. The FCOM cases (engines 1 and 2 running, taxi, flight, engines 3 and 4 running) are all covered by that one rule, so the same rule is used here.-
The A32NX has no cargo door inhibition.Design choice: the inhibition is in the A380X hydraulics (the green electric pumps). The A32NX only gets the full-open fix (next feature).-
A door that is not closed at engine start stays open until the engines are stopped.Without the green electric pumps there is no power to move it (FCOM DSC-52-30-40-10).Stop the engines.
The flyPad row shows the new state only with core/efb-redesign.The row is a row of the Material 3 Services page, which is on that branch.Merge both branches.

How to use

  1. Stop on the ground and stop the engines (the green electric pumps are then allowed).
  2. flyPad > Ground > Services > Doors: switch "Cargo fwd + aft" on to open both cargo doors, off to close them.
  3. With an engine running, expect the doors to stay closed (the row says so); stop the engines first.

2. Cargo doors open fully and are held locked open

Aircraft: A380X and A32NX  |  Where: Systems (cargo door controller, both aircraft); flyPad > Ground > Services  |  Status: A380X: sim-tested OK by the user with GSX cargo loading (2026-10-17). A32NX: not sim-tested. Rust tests: 1 per aircraft (opens fully, stays open after the pumps stop, then closes and locks).

What it is for

An opened cargo door stopped near 90 % and then sagged back to about 88 % for good (A380X 87.7 %, A32NX 88.8 %). GSX starts loading above 80 %, so on the A380X its ULD loader went through the door. The real door is mechanically locked at the end of its travel. The doors of both aircraft now open fully and are locked open there.

NoteA380 FCOM DSC-52-30-40-10 P2: the cargo door has a "FULLY OPEN AND ARRESTED" indicator.

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
Engines stopped, a cargo door is asked to openThe door opens to 98 % and is locked open there. A380X: about 50 s after the request; A32NX: about 25 s.
Door open, the hydraulic pumps stopThe door stays at 98 %, locked open. Before: it stopped near 88 % and GSX loaders went through it.
Door locked open, the close requestThe door unlocks, closes and locks down (within about a minute), as before.
Door locked open, the operation inhibited (A380X, engine running)The door stays locked open; a close request is carried out once the engines are stopped.
A380X flyPad, row "Cargo fwd + aft""Moving" with a progress bar up to 98 %, "Open" from 98 % (was 90 %; needs core/efb-redesign).

How to use

  1. Stop the engines (A380X: the green electric pumps are then allowed).
  2. Open the cargo doors: A380X flyPad > Ground > Services > Doors, row "Cargo fwd + aft"; A32NX flyPad > Ground > Services, cargo door button (or the exterior door interactive point of the sim).
  3. Wait until the door is fully open before GSX cargo loading starts.
