1. VD runways drawn as flat ground

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: VD terrain profile  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user (2026-10-03): OK at VHHH; one short spike left at CYUL, accepted.

What it is for

The MSFS terrain map draws the airport diagram in light grey over the terrain, and no setting hides it. The VD read that grey as terrain far above its plot: spikes wherever the vertical cut crossed an airport, the destination on approach included.

  • The systems host searches the airports within 25 NM in the sim's airport database and publishes up to 40 runways, closest first, as rectangles: the runway length and width plus 0.15 NM on each side, at the higher threshold elevation.
  • Where the cut crosses such a rectangle, the VD draws flat ground at the runway elevation instead of the terrain map.
  • Rectangles around the runways (not a circle around the airport) keep the real terrain next to the runways.

How to use
