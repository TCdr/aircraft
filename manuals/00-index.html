2. How to read them

  • What it is for: the problem the change solves, in one paragraph.
  • Using it / numbered steps: where the controls are and what to press.
  • What you should see tables: the expected behaviour in each situation; if the sim does something else, it is a bug worth reporting with the PR number.
  • Limitations and why: what does not work yet, the reason (sim limit, missing data, not in the FCOM, out of scope) and what to do about it.
  • Stacked PRs need the PRs below them installed: the branch line of each manual names them.
  • Pictures that do not come from the harness (drawn diagrams, in-sim screenshots taken during development) carry an amber line under their caption: they are not representative of the in-game UI and serve as a reference for the explanation only.
