2. Stacks: what must be merged first

Most branches are based directly on master (FlyByWire master 255546cb0 merged in) and can be merged in any order. The others are stacked: a branch needs every branch under it merged first.

4. How to read them

  • Each manual has one numbered section per feature of its branch. The grey line under the feature title gives the aircraft, where to find it and its status (sim-tested, or not, with the date).
  • What it is for: the problem the change solves, in a few sentences.
  • How to use: numbered steps for normal use.
  • How to test: a table of steps a tester follows in the sim, each with the expected result. If the sim does something else, it is a bug worth reporting with the branch name.
  • What you should see tables: the expected behaviour in each situation; Limitations and why: what does not work yet, the reason and what to do about it.
  • Pictures that do not come from the harness (drawn diagrams, in-sim screenshots taken during development) carry an amber line under their caption: they are not representative of the in-game UI and serve as a reference for the explanation only.
