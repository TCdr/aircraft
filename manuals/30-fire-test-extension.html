FIRE TEST extended after the button release

A flyPad option on both aircraft: the FIRE TEST goes on for 7 seconds after you release the button, so you can check every warning and light without holding it
AircraftA32NX and A380X Where to find itflyPad > Settings > Realism: "Extend Fire Test Warnings After Button Release" PR branchpr/30-fire-test-extension StatusA32NX tested in the sim (2026-09-17); A380X tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-09-29). PicturesflyPad page (A380X) in the local browser harness, running the build installed in the sim.

1. What it is for

On the aircraft, the FIRE TEST lasts as long as the button is held. In the simulator, holding the button with the mouse while looking at the ECAM, the master warning and every FIRE and AGENT light is awkward. This option keeps the test running for 7 seconds after you release the button. It is marked unrealistic and is on by default.

2. What it does

AircraftTestKept 7 s after the release
A32NXAPU, ENG 1 and ENG 2 FIRE TEST pushbuttonsmaster warning and CRC, ECAM, FIRE pushbutton lights, AGENT / SQUIB DISCH lights, together
A380XFIRE TEST pushbutton (overhead)the fire detection test itself: FIRE lights, squib lights and the FWS warnings, together

Option off: the test stops the instant you release the button, as on the aircraft.

3. What to expect

  • The test starts half a second after you press (A380X), as before.
  • Pressing again during the 7 seconds starts a new 7 seconds after the next release.
  • The A380X systems host and fire protection system changed: a full MSFS restart after an update.
