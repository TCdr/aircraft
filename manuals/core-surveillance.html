1. A380X SURV controls, STATUS &amp; SWITCHING and the pedestal SURV panel

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: MFD SURV pages; pedestal SURV panel  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-09-19: pedestal buttons and MFD pages switch in step; WXR buttons drive the radar).

What it is for

The weather radar block of the MFD SURV CONTROLS page, the STATUS & SWITCHING system selection and the pedestal SURV panel buttons were stubs. They now work and drive the same systems.

What it is for

The A380 has no separate radar, TCAS or TAWS control panels: they are on the MFD SURV pages and the pedestal SURV panel (FCOM DSC-34-20-60-50). In FBW the weather radar block of the page was a stub (every button greyed), STATUS & SWITCHING did not reflect failures, and no button of the pedestal panel worked. Now they do, and the page and the panel stay in step.

SURV CONTROLS

WXR buttonEffect
WXR AUTO / OFFWeather radar on or off (the ND radar picture of core/nd-weather-terrain).
PRED W/S AUTO / OFFPredictive windshear (written for the systems; MSFS has no predictive windshear).
TURB AUTO / OFFTurbulence detection (magenta on the ND with the native radar of core/nd-weather-terrain).

The WXR buttons are inhibited only when the selected WXR system has really failed.

STATUS &amp; SWITCHING

  • WXR DISPLAY, TURB, PRED W/S, TERR SYS, GPWS items show the real failure of each system.
  • XPDR / TCAS SYS 1 / SYS 2 select the transponder system (the one the TCAS uses) and light from it.

The pedestal SURV panel

The panel buttons never worked: their light template runs the button code only when the panel is powered, and the pedestal never told it so. They are powered from DC 1 now and linked to the MFD pages:

Each button lights from the same value the page uses, so pressing one on the pedestal changes the page, and the reverse.

How to use

  1. Open the SURV pages: MFD system menu SURV (or the KCCU SURV key), then CONTROLS or STATUS & SWITCHING.
  2. On CONTROLS set the WXR (WXR, PRED W/S, TURB, GAIN, MODE, WX ON VD, ELEVN/TILT), the TCAS (TA/RA, TA ONLY, STBY; NORM, ABV, BLW) and the TAWS options.
  3. On STATUS & SWITCHING, or with the pedestal buttons, select WXR / TAWS SYS 1 or SYS 2 and XPDR / TCAS SYS 1 or SYS 2.
  4. Use the pedestal TCAS ABV / BLW / TA ONLY and G/S MODE buttons as shortcuts: the MFD page follows.

2. A380X VD: separate NO TERR / NO WX DATA messages

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: Vertical display (VD) under the ND  |  Status: Tested in the sim (September 2026).

What it is for

TERR SYS OFF removed both the terrain and the weather from the VD message logic, so the VD always said "NO TERR AND WX DATA AVAILABLE". Each source now has its own message.

Messages on the vertical display

The VD message was always "NO TERR AND WX DATA AVAILABLE" when TERR SYS was off. Now:

How to use

  1. Nothing to set: the VD message follows the TERR SYS, WXR and WX ON VD buttons of MFD > SURV > CONTROLS.

How to test

3. A380X ECAM procedures: WXR &amp; TAWS and XPDR &amp; TCAS lines sensed

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: E/WD procedures; MFD SURV STATUS & SWITCHING  |  Status: Tested in the sim (September 2026).

What it is for

The dual ADR and IR fault procedures ask the crew to move the WXR & TAWS and the XPDR & TCAS to the system that still has valid data. Those lines were hard-coded as done, so the ECAM ticked them even when the wrong system stayed selected. They now follow the selection made on the MFD SURV STATUS & SWITCHING page, and the TCAS 1 / 2 FAULT procedures show their XPDR & TCAS line, which was hidden.

4. A380X ECAM LINE-UP checklist: CABIN CREW line

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: E/WD normal checklist LINE-UP  |  Status: Tested in the sim (September 2026).

What it is for

The A380 FCOM BEFORE TAKEOFF checklist has a CABIN CREW item; the ECAM LINE-UP checklist did not.

LINE-UP checklist

The ECAM LINE-UP checklist gets the CABIN CREW line that the flyPad checklist already had (A380 FCOM BEFORE TAKEOFF checklist: CABIN CREW ... ADVISE). It is ticked by the crew.

5. TCAS: intruders shown and announced as TAs while RAs are inhibited

Aircraft: A32NX and A380X  |  Where: ND traffic display; TCAS mode  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-09-29).

What it is for

With every RA inhibited (TA ONLY selected, or low: below 900 ft radio altitude in descent, until 1100 ft in climb, see the next feature), the TCAS kept classifying an intruder that met the RA criteria as an RA. The ND drew it as a red RA square, and since it was not a TA, there was no "TRAFFIC, TRAFFIC" and no TA state. A real TCAS in TA ONLY shows it as an amber TA with the TA aural. The classification is now capped at TA while all RAs are inhibited.

What you see

SituationWhat you should see
TA ONLY selected, intruder meeting the RA criteriaAmber TA circle and "TRAFFIC, TRAFFIC" (before: red RA square, no aural).
Below 900 ft radio altitude in descent, until 1100 ft in climb (all RAs inhibited)Same: TAs only, TA ONLY on the ND.
Partial inhibits (no CLIMB / DESCEND / INCREASE DESCENT RA)Unchanged: RAs are still issued.
An RA already running when the inhibit startsIt runs to its own end.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
Same change on both aircraft.The A32NX TcasComputer and the A380X LegacyTcasComputer share the classification code.-

How to use

  1. Select TA ONLY: A32NX ATC/TCAS panel TCAS mode selector to TA; A380X MFD > SURV > CONTROLS TCAS TA ONLY (or the pedestal TA ONLY button).
  2. Traffic that would give an RA is shown as an amber TA with the "TRAFFIC, TRAFFIC" aural.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1In flight above 1100 ft radio altitude with traffic (AI or multiplayer) converging on you. TCAS in TA/RA.An intruder meeting the RA criteria is a red square with an RA aural.
2Select TA ONLY (A32NX: TCAS mode selector TA; A380X: SURV CONTROLS TA ONLY).TA ONLY on the ND. The same intruder is now an amber TA circle with "TRAFFIC, TRAFFIC" (before: a red RA square and no aural).
3Back to TA/RA during an RA already running, then TA ONLY again.An RA already running when the inhibition starts runs to its own end.

6. TCAS: low-altitude RA and TA aural inhibitions with the real hysteresis

Aircraft: A32NX and A380X  |  Where: ND traffic display (TA ONLY message); TCAS aurals  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-09-29, with the TA ONLY change).

What it is for

The TCAS inhibits all RAs close to the ground, and lower down the TA aural too. Both used a flat threshold (1000 ft and 500 ft radio altitude). The A380 FCTM (Supplementary Information, TCAS) gives a hysteresis, the FCOM "below 1000 ft ±100 ft". Both TCAS computers now use it:

InhibitionStarts (descending through)Ends (climbing through)
All RAs (TA ONLY on the ND)900 ft radio altitude1100 ft radio altitude
TA aural ("TRAFFIC, TRAFFIC")400 ft radio altitude600 ft radio altitude
  • Without a valid radio altitude, neither low-altitude inhibition applies.
  • The four altitudes are named constants of the TCAS code (no other effect).

How to use

  1. Nothing to set: the inhibitions follow the radio altitude.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A32NX or A380X on approach, TCAS in TA/RA, descending through 1000 ft radio altitude. Watch the ND.No TA ONLY message yet at 1000 ft.
2Continue below 900 ft radio altitude.TA ONLY appears on the ND (all RAs inhibited).
3Go around and climb.TA ONLY stays up to 1100 ft radio altitude and disappears above it.
4With traffic close by on a low approach (AI or multiplayer), descend below 400 ft radio altitude.The TA symbol stays, the "TRAFFIC, TRAFFIC" aural is not given; it is given again above 600 ft in the climb.

7. A32NX: TCAS message centred at the bottom of the ND

Aircraft: A32NX  |  Where: ND, bottom centre  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-09-24; again 2026-09-29 with the TCAS changes).

What it is for

The shared TCAS / WXR message component put the TCAS message ("TA ONLY", "TCAS", "TCAS STBY") at the left of its line. The A320 ND shows it at the bottom centre, below the aircraft symbol between the ADF and VOR fields (A320 FCOM DSC-34-SURV-60-20, ND indications figure), where the A380X already placed it. The A32NX ND now centres it the same way (see the A32NX picture in the TA feature above).

How to use

  1. Nothing to set: the message shows wherever the TCAS sends one.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A32NX, ND in ROSE NAV or ARC. Set the TCAS mode selector on the ATC/TCAS panel to TA.TA ONLY at the bottom centre of the ND, below the aircraft symbol, between the ADF/VOR fields (before: at the left).
2Set the TCAS mode selector to STBY.TCAS STBY at the same centred place.
