1. Exterior spoilers move by panel group

Aircraft: A32NX  |  Where: Outside view of the wings  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-03).

What it is for

Each wing has five spoiler panels: 2 to 5 help the ailerons in roll, 2 to 4 are the speedbrakes, and all five are ground spoilers (A320 FCOM DSC-27). The exterior model moved all the panels of a wing together from the wing average, so spoiler 1 rose in turns and with the speedbrakes. The panels now follow their own deflection from the hydraulic model, by group, and the angle is shown in true degrees.

  • Three groups per wing: panel 1, panels 2 + 3, panels 4 + 5. Panels 2 and 3 share their skin joints in the model, and so do panels 4 and 5, so a group shows the mean of its two panels.
  • True degrees: the model animation goes to 45 degrees; the ground spoilers (50 degrees) show as the model maximum.

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
Roll in flightThe panels 2 to 5 of the down-going wing rise (as two groups); spoiler 1 stays down.
Speedbrakes extendedPanels 2 to 4 rise; spoiler 1 stays down; the 4 + 5 group shows the mean of panels 4 and 5 (half way when only panel 4 is out).
Ground spoilers at landing or rejected takeoffAll three groups rise to the model maximum.

How to use

  1. Nothing to set: fly as usual and watch the wings from the outside view or the cabin.
