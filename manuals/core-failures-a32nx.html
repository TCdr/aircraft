5. Fire detection model and fire failures

Aircraft: A32NX  |  Where: flyPad > Failures > ATA 26 Fire Protection; overhead FIRE panel; ENG MASTER panel; E/WD, STATUS  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK. An agent putting out an MSFS failures menu fire: not sim-tested (Rust fire protection tests).

What it is for

Nine new failures: Engine 1 fire, Engine 2 fire, APU fire, and Engine 1 fire detection loop A, Engine 1 fire detection loop B, Engine 2 fire detection loop A, Engine 2 fire detection loop B, APU fire detection loop A, APU fire detection loop B.

Each zone now has the two detection loops and the fire detection unit of the aircraft (A320 FCOM DSC-26-20-10). A fire warning comes when both loops detect the fire, when one loop detects it and the other one is failed, or when both loops break within 5 s (flame effect). The logic moved from the A380X into the common code; the A380X behaviour is unchanged. The fires of the MSFS failures menu still give the warnings, through the loops; an AGENT discharge (or the automatic APU discharge) can also put such a fire out, which ends the ENG / APU FIRE warning, and the fire stays out while the MSFS failure stays on (both aircraft).   • The ENG MASTER panel FIRE light comes on with the engine fire warning (before: FIRE TEST only).
  • APU fire on the ground: the APU shuts down and the APU AGENT discharges automatically 3 s after the warning (FCOM DSC-26-20-20).
  • Each AGENT discharge has one chance in two to put a flyPad fire (or an MSFS failures menu fire) out.
  • Loop supplies (FCOM PRO-ABN-ELEC INOP SYS): DC BUS 2 for ENG 1 LOOP B and ENG 2 LOOP A, DC ESS BUS for ENG 1 LOOP A and ENG 2 LOOP B, DC BAT BUS for both APU loops. An unpowered loop is a failed loop.

What it is for

Six new failures: Engine 1 oil leak, Engine 2 oil leak, Engine 1 oil filter clog, Engine 2 oil filter clog, Engine 1 oil overheat and Engine 2 oil overheat, with the oil indications and the ECAM alerts of the A320 FCOM (DSC-70-90-40, PRO-ABN-ENG).

The FADEC computes the oil quantity, pressure and temperature again: its oil model had been dead since a FADEC migration and is restored (from the A380X FADEC, with the A32NX engine imbalance effect on the oil pressure). The failures act on that model:

  • Leak: the oil system empties at 2 qt per minute of simulation time (the SD tank reading falls about 1.6 qt/min). The pump keeps its normal pressure down to 2.5 qt in the whole system and gives none at 0.6 qt, so the pressure falls once the tank reads nearly empty.
  • Filter clog: CLOG on the SD ENGINE page and the ENG OIL FILTER CLOG caution, no other effect.
  • Overheat: the oil goes to 135 °C at idle and 175 °C at 100 % N2 (about 165 °C at cruise), with a 60 s time constant: ENG OIL HI TEMP after about 2 minutes in cruise; THR LEVER IDLE brings it below 155 °C within about 30 s. Once the failure is cleared the oil cools down gradually to its normal temperature.
  • SD ENGINE page: the FCOM 0-22 QT scale; the quantity pulses below 3.25 QT (until 4.75 QT); the oil pressure red below 13 PSI, pulsing between 13 and 16 PSI with N2 above 75 % (until it is back above 20 PSI); amber CLOG under the oil pressure. The CRUISE page shows the same FADEC oil quantity.

The overheat stopped far below its target because MSFS pulls the oil temperature back to its own model: the FADEC now keeps its own overheat temperature.
  • The start N2 and fuel flow of the FADEC model also blend smoothly at low N2 now (they overshot below about 10 % N2).

What it is for

The FBW FADEC ran the engine model but not what a real FADEC does during a start. The systems now run the start sequence of the A320 FCOM (DSC-70-80-30, DSC-70-80-40):

  • Igniters: one igniter for a first automatic start on the ground, alternated between starts; both for a new attempt, a manual start and an in-flight start. Continuous ignition with the ENG MODE selector at IGN/START (one igniter, both if it has failed), with the green IGNITION memo. Since the FADEC also selects the continuous ignition by itself while the engine anti-ice is on (see "FCOM fixes: FADEC relight, automatic ignition..."). The SD igniter indication now comes from this sequence.
  • Automatic start: ignition from 16 % N2, fuel (HP valve) at 22 % N2, ignition off and start valve closed at 50 % N2.   • The start code shared with the A380X got two settings (A380X engine alerts); the A32NX keeps its values (no light-up after 18 s also in flight, LO START AIR PRESS for a ground start only): nothing changes for the A32NX.
  • The SD ENGINE page shows the start indications at CRANK too.
  • Tuning: an engine that is OFF only shows running once MSFS really burns fuel in it.

Manual cabin pressure control (MAN V/S CTL)

When both pressure controllers are lost (CAB PR SYS 1+2 FAULT: MODE SEL MAN, MAN V/S CTL AS RQRD, FCOM; in a DC EMER CONFIG test the cabin climbed at about 5,500 ft/min with CAB PR EXCESS CAB ALT), the crew drives the outflow valve with the MAN V/S CTL switch: UP opens the valve (cabin climbs), DN closes it (cabin descends) (FCOM). The manual motor, on the DC BAT BUS, moves the valve over its full travel in 55 s (pressure_valve.rs). In cruise the valve is only a few percent open, so the cabin V/S reacts strongly: about 1,800 ft/min per second of input in the model (an estimate).

  • Use short taps of 0.2 to 0.5 s, then wait and watch the cabin V/S on the SD CAB PRESS page: "only short inputs should be applied" (FCOM), and the valve change can take 10 s to show.
  • Never hold the switch to drive the valve to a stop in cruise: the cabin climbs or dives by thousands of ft/min.
  • Targets from the STATUS page: climb 500 ft/min, descent 300 ft/min; cabin altitude by flight level (FL390 8,000 ft ... FL250 3,000 ft); in final approach MAN V/S CTL FULL UP, delta P zero before opening the doors.
  • To recover: MODE SEL back to AUTO once a controller works again (e.g. after the electrical failure is removed): the automatic system takes the valve back. MAN for at least 10 s then AUTO also selects the other system (FCOM).

IGNITION memo and the SD IGN indication

The green IGNITION memo shows "when continuous ignition is activated on any engine" (FCOM): ENG MODE selector at IGN/START with the engine running, the FADEC automatic ignition, or the engine anti-ice on this branch. The SD ENGINE page shows the igniters (IGN A / B), the start valve and the PSI only with the ENG MODE selector at IGN/START or CRANK (StartIndications.ts; the ENGINE page comes at the start sequence or at CRANK, FCOM). So with the selector at NORM and the continuous ignition on (engine anti-ice), the SD shows no IGN letters: the E/WD memo is the indication.

NoteKnown issue: in a sim test the IGNITION memo did not show although the continuous ignition variable was on. The cause is not found yet.

An engine keeps running after its ENG FIRE pushbutton

The ENG FIRE pushbutton closes the LP fuel valve (FCOM); the engine then runs on the fuel left between the valve and the nozzles and stops by itself, "after a time delay up to 2 min 30 s" (FCOM, on this branch through the LP valve model of core/fire). How long it runs on depends on the engine state (thrust, airport altitude): in the model about 1 min 40 s at a sea-level idle, never more than 2 min 30 s. The FCOM ENG FIRE procedure sets the ENG MASTER OFF first, which closes the LP and HP valves and stops the combustion without run-on, then pushes the FIRE pushbutton.

A/THR without a V2

The A/THR arms at FLX/TOGA only when SRS engages, and SRS needs a V2: "If V2 is not inserted, the speed reference system (SRS) will not engage for takeoff" (FCOM). Without V2 the A/THR stays off at take-off, V/S engages 5 s after lift-off and the current speed becomes the FCU target. This is the FlyByWire FMGC code (FmgcComputer.cpp, unchanged from FlyByWire master), the same on every branch.

  1. Normal: enter V2 on the MCDU PERF TAKE OFF page (with the other speeds): the A/THR arms at FLX/TOGA.
  2. Without V2, at ACC ALT (FCOM TAKEOFF WITH NO V2 ENTRY): push the A/THR pushbutton on the FCU, set the thrust levers to the CL detent, push the SPD knob for a managed speed (or select the climb speed and pull it).
  3. If the A/THR still does not engage (seen after a quick runway start without V2): push or pull the FCU SPD knob first so that a speed target exists, then push the A/THR pushbutton.
