1. Clearer failure names

Aircraft: A32NX  |  Where: flyPad > Failures  |  Status: Not sim-tested (labels only).

What it is for

The failure names were audited ATA by ATA against what the systems code fails. Three names were misleading: the ATA 21 valve is the hot air pressure regulating valve, the FMGC failures only fail the flight guidance part of the FMGC, and the RA failures are antenna failures. Only the labels change.

2. Transponder failures

Aircraft: A32NX  |  Where: flyPad > Failures > ATA 34 Navigation; pedestal ATC/TCAS panel; E/WD, STATUS  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-04).

What it is for

Two new failures, ATC/XPDR 1 and ATC/XPDR 2. MSFS has a single transponder: it stands for the transponder selected on the ATC panel (SYS 1 / SYS 2). When the selected one fails, the sim transponder stops replying and the ATC FAIL light comes on; selecting the other system brings the replies back. With both lost, the TCAS goes to standby. The ECAM alerts and STATUS follow the A320 FCOM PRO-ABN-NAV procedures.

Fix on the way: the ATC/XPDR STBY indication read a second sim transponder that does not exist; the standby state of each transponder now comes from the system selection and the mode selector.

SYS 2 selectedATC FAIL off, replies again; the procedure line is done.
ATC/XPDR 2 failedThe same with NAV ATC/XPDR 2 FAULT, -ATC/XPDR ...... SYS 1, INOP SYS ATC/XPDR 2, ADS-B RPTG 2.
Both failedNAV ATC/XPDR 1+2 FAULT (crew awareness); STATUS INOP SYS: TCAS, ATC/XPDR 1, ATC/XPDR 2, ADS-B RPTG 1, ADS-B RPTG 2; the TCAS goes to standby.

3. RMP and VHF failures

Aircraft: A32NX  |  Where: flyPad > Failures > ATA 23 Communication; pedestal RMP 1 and RMP 2  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-04).

What it is for

Five new ATA 23 failures: RMP 1, RMP 2, VHF 1, VHF 2 and VHF 3 (same ids as the A380X ATA 23 failures). A failed RMP goes blank and stops tuning; the other RMP still tunes every VHF (A320 FCOM DSC-23-10-20: "If one RMP fails, the remaining one controls all the VHF transceivers"). A failed VHF switches its MSFS COM radio off: it neither receives nor transmits.

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
RMP 1 (or 2) failedBoth frequency displays blank, the green key lights and SEL off, the keys and knobs do nothing (FCOM DSC-23-60 failure cases).
The other RMPStill tunes VHF 1, 2 and 3.
VHF 1 (2, 3) failedCOM 1 (2, 3) silent: no reception, no transmission. The RMPs still show and change its frequency.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
No ECAM alert for these failures.Not part of this change.Check the RMP and the radio.
A NAV key left on on a failed RMP still holds the FMGC navaid tuning.As with an RMP switched off: the navaid tuner (shared with the A380X) reads the NAV key only.Avoid the NAV key before failing an RMP.
No RMP 3 and no audio control panel / AMU failure.RMP 3 is not in the A32NX cockpit, and the MSFS radios have no audio channel to fail.None.

How to use

  1. Open flyPad > Failures > ATA 23 Communication and activate RMP 1, RMP 2, VHF 1, VHF 2 or VHF 3.
  2. With an RMP failed, tune the radios from the other RMP.

4. FWC 1 and FWC 2 failures

Aircraft: A32NX  |  Where: flyPad > Failures > ATA 31 Indicating / Recording System; E/WD, STATUS  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-04).

What it is for

Two new failures, FWC 1 and FWC 2 (A320 FCOM DSC-31-05-30, PRO-ABN-FWS). With one FWC failed, the other one keeps the warning system working and shows the fault. With both failed, the ECAM alerts, memos, STATUS, MASTER WARN / CAUT lights, aurals (including the STALL aural) and the altitude callouts are lost, and the E/WD shows the FWC 1+2 FAULT display. Each FWC also follows its own electrical supply.

5. Fire detection model and fire failures

Aircraft: A32NX  |  Where: flyPad > Failures > ATA 26 Fire Protection; overhead FIRE panel; ENG MASTER panel; E/WD, STATUS  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-04).

What it is for

Nine new failures: Engine 1 fire, Engine 2 fire, APU fire, and Engine 1 fire detection loop A, Engine 1 fire detection loop B, Engine 2 fire detection loop A, Engine 2 fire detection loop B, APU fire detection loop A, APU fire detection loop B.

Each zone now has the two detection loops and the fire detection unit of the aircraft (A320 FCOM DSC-26-20-10). A fire warning comes when both loops detect the fire, when one loop detects it and the other one is failed, or when both loops break within 5 s (flame effect). The logic moved from the A380X into the common code; the A380X behaviour is unchanged. The fires of the MSFS failures menu still give the warnings, through the loops. The FIRE TEST is unchanged.

  • The ENG MASTER panel FIRE light comes on with the engine fire warning (before: FIRE TEST only).
  • APU fire on the ground: the APU shuts down and the APU AGENT discharges automatically 3 s after the warning (FCOM DSC-26-20-20).
  • Each AGENT discharge has one chance in two to put a flyPad fire out.
  • Loop supplies (FCOM PRO-ABN-ELEC INOP SYS): DC BUS 2 for ENG 1 LOOP B and ENG 2 LOOP A, DC ESS BUS for ENG 1 LOOP A and ENG 2 LOOP B, DC BAT BUS for both APU loops. An unpowered loop is a failed loop.

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
Engine 1 fireENG 1 FIRE warning (from core/fire), red ENG 1 FIRE pushbutton light and red FIRE light on the ENG MASTER panel.
APU fire on the groundAPU FIRE warning; the APU shuts down; 3 s later the APU AGENT discharges (DISCH light).
AGENT dischargedThe fire goes out, or not (one chance in two per discharge).
One loop failedAmber ENG 1 FIRE LOOP A FAULT (LOOP B, ENG 2, APU FIRE LOOP A / B); STATUS INOP SYS ENG 1 LOOP A (ENG 1 LOOP B, ENG 2 LOOP A / B, APU LOOP A / B). A fire is still detected by the other loop.
Both loops of a zone failedENG 1 FIRE DET FAULT (ENG 2, APU FIRE DET FAULT) instead of the two loop cautions; STATUS INOP SYS FIRE DET 1 (FIRE DET 2, APU FIRE DET).

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
FIRE DET FAULT also comes from a failed fire detection unit.See the next feature, "Fire detection unit failures".-
The LOOP and DET FAULT cautions are inhibited during the takeoff and the landing (phases 3, 4, 5, 7, 8).The FCOM gives the inhibition as a figure: that of the other crew awareness cautions is used.Test at the gate or in cruise.
A full MSFS restart is needed after an update.The systems WASM module and the cockpit behaviour XML change.Restart the sim after installing.

How to use

  1. Open flyPad > Failures > ATA 26 Fire Protection and activate a fire or a loop failure.
  2. Fight a fire with the ECAM procedure (FIRE pushbutton, AGENT 1, AGENT 2). Deactivating the failure also ends the fire.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A32NX on the ground, engines running. Activate Engine 1 fire.ENG 1 FIRE warning, master warning, continuous chime, red light in the ENG 1 FIRE pushbutton and on the ENG MASTER 1 panel.
2Follow the ECAM: ENG MASTER 1 OFF, push the ENG 1 FIRE pushbutton, press AGENT 1.AGENT 1 DISCH; the fire goes out or not (one chance in two). If not, AGENT 2 gives another chance.
3Deactivate the failure, reset the FIRE pushbutton. With the APU running, activate APU fire.APU FIRE warning; the APU shuts down; about 3 s later the APU AGENT discharges by itself.
4Deactivate it. Activate Engine 2 fire detection loop A.Amber ENG 2 FIRE LOOP A FAULT; STATUS INOP SYS ENG 2 LOOP A.
5Activate Engine 2 fire with loop A still failed.ENG 2 FIRE warning: loop B alone detects the fire.
6Deactivate the fire, activate Engine 2 fire detection loop B as well.The loop cautions are replaced by ENG 2 FIRE DET FAULT; STATUS INOP SYS FIRE DET 2.
7Optional: start an engine fire from the MSFS failures menu.The ENG FIRE warning still comes (through the loops).

6. Fire detection unit failures, FIRE TEST light logic, APU fire horn

Aircraft: A32NX  |  Where: flyPad > Failures > ATA 26 Fire Protection; overhead FIRE panel; ENG MASTER panel; E/WD, STATUS  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-04); the horn sound itself is silent in MSFS 2024. Rust tests (fire_protection), unit tests: FireDetectionFaults.spec.ts, EngineMasterFireLight.spec.ts.

What it is for

Three follow-ups of the fire detection model above:

  • Fire detection unit failures: three new failures, Engine 1 fire detection unit (FDU), Engine 2 fire detection unit (FDU) and APU fire detection unit (FDU). A failed FDU gives the FIRE DET FAULT caution of its zone even with healthy loops, and detects no fire. Clearing the failure with a fire present gives the fire warning at once.
  • ENG MASTER panel FIRE light during the FIRE TEST: the A320 FCOM (DSC-26-20-20, ENG 1(2) FIRE TEST pb) says that the FIRE lights do not come on with an engine 1 loop A fault or an engine 2 loop B fault. The light now stays dark in those cases (a loop fault is its failure or the loss of its DC ESS supply).
  • APU fire external horn: an APU fire on the ground sounds the horn in the nose gear bay (FCOM DSC-26-20-20 APU FIRE light, DSC-49-10-20), on the same sound as the MECH call; not during the FIRE TEST.

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
Engine 1 FDU failedAmber ENG 1 FIRE DET FAULT (ENG 2 FIRE DET FAULT, APU FIRE DET FAULT for the other units), no LOOP FAULT caution (a failed FDU cannot tell which loop is failed); STATUS INOP SYS FIRE DET 1 (FIRE DET 2, APU FIRE DET).
A fire in a zone with its FDU failedNo fire warning.
FDU failure cleared while the fire burnsThe fire warning comes at once.
ENG 1 FIRE TEST with Engine 1 fire detection loop A failedThe ENG MASTER 1 FIRE light stays dark; the rest of the test as before.
ENG 2 FIRE TEST with Engine 2 fire detection loop B failedThe ENG MASTER 2 FIRE light stays dark.
APU fire on the groundThe external horn variable is set (L:A32NX_FIRE_APU_EXTERNAL_HORN); the horn sound plays at the nose gear.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
The APU fire horn is not heard in MSFS 2024.The Asobo horn sound it uses is silent in MSFS 2024 (the MECH call too).Check the L:var with a SimConnect / variable tool.
No APU SHUT OFF pb and no APU FIRE light on the external power panel.The exterior model has no such panel.None.
A full MSFS restart is needed after an update.The systems WASM module, the cockpit behaviour XML and the sound.xml change.Restart the sim after installing.

How to use

  1. Open flyPad > Failures > ATA 26 Fire Protection and activate an "... fire detection unit (FDU)" failure.
  2. Follow the ECAM (crew awareness); deactivate the failure to restore the unit.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A32NX at the gate, powered, no failure. Activate Engine 1 fire detection unit (FDU).Amber ENG 1 FIRE DET FAULT, no ENG 1 FIRE LOOP A / B FAULT; STATUS INOP SYS FIRE DET 1.
2Also activate Engine 1 fire.No ENG 1 FIRE warning.
3Deactivate Engine 1 fire detection unit (FDU).The ENG 1 FIRE warning comes at once. Deactivate the fire.
4Repeat with Engine 2 fire detection unit (FDU) and APU fire detection unit (FDU).ENG 2 FIRE DET FAULT / STATUS FIRE DET 2; APU FIRE DET FAULT / STATUS APU FIRE DET.

6Deactivate it, activate Engine 2 fire detection loop B, hold the ENG 2 FIRE TEST pb.The ENG MASTER 2 FIRE light stays dark. With loop A of engine 2 failed instead, it comes on.

7. Wing tank pump and X FEED valve failures

Aircraft: A32NX  |  Where: flyPad > Failures > ATA 28 Fuel; overhead FUEL panel; SD FUEL  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-04), after the fix that holds the MSFS pump / valve switch.

What it is for

Five new failures: L TK pump 1, L TK pump 2, R TK pump 1, R TK pump 2 and X FEED valve jammed in place. The FUEL panel pushbuttons now set a selection, and the systems host moves the MSFS pump or valve switch from it: a failed pump is held off, the jammed valve is held where it was. The FAULT lights, the SD FUEL page and the cautions compare the selection with the real state (A320 FCOM PRO-ABN-FUEL).

  • SD FUEL page: amber LO only for a pump that is ON with a low pressure; the X FEED valve is amber when it disagrees with its pushbutton (FCOM colours).
  • The FUEL X FEED memo now needs the X FEED pushbutton ON and the valve not fully closed.

8. Centre tank transfer valve failures and fuel follow-ups

Aircraft: A32NX  |  Where: flyPad > Failures > ATA 28 Fuel; overhead FUEL panel; SD FUEL; flyPad checklists  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-04). Unit tests: FuelPumpValveFaults.spec.ts, FuelPumpsAndValves.spec.ts, FuelPumpValveIndications.spec.ts, CheckItemStates.spec.ts.

What it is for

Two new failures, CTR TK L XFR valve jammed in place and CTR TK R XFR valve jammed in place: the centre tank transfer valve of that side stays open or closed, whatever its CTR TK XFR pushbutton commands. The FWC raises the A320 FCOM PRO-ABN-FUEL cautions: FUEL CTR L(R) XFR FAULT (valve not fully closed, or not fully open) and, with both valves stuck the same way, FUEL CTR L + R XFR FAULT, which replaces the two single ones. The SD FUEL page shows the valve amber and boxes the centre tank quantity.

  • The centre tank fuel only transfers while an inner tank pump of the same wing runs: the jet pumps are driven by the wing tank pumps (FCOM DSC-28-10). Switching the wing pumps off stops the centre tank transfer.
  • flyPad checklist: the securing / parking checklist item FUEL PUMPS ... OFF now reads the four wing tank pump pushbuttons, not whether the pumps run (the CTR TK XFR pushbuttons are items of their own in the FCOM).

What it is for

Four new failures in a new flyPad chapter, ATA 72 Engine. A flameout cuts the engine fuel once: the crew can relight the engine with the ENG MASTER OFF then ON, inside the in-flight relight envelope. A seizure also stops the core: no relight while the failure is active. The FWC gives the A320 FCOM ENG 1(2) FAIL and ENG 1(2) SHUT DOWN alerts with their STATUS, and the flyPad failures page now describes the ATA 28 Fuel and ATA 72 Engine chapters like the others.

IdflyPad failure (ATA 72 Engine)What the code does
72000Engine 1 flameout (crew relight possible)Cuts the engine 1 fuel (latched). The engine runs down and windmills; the FADEC does not relight it. ENG MASTER 1 OFF then ON inside the relight envelope relights it; it then runs normally while the failure stays listed. Deactivate and activate it again for a new flameout.
72001Engine 2 flameout (crew relight possible)The same for engine 2.
72010Engine 1 seizure (no relight)Cuts the fuel; the core (N2) stops in about 3 s while N1 windmills. No relight while the failure is active; once it is cleared the engine relights by the normal procedure.
72011Engine 2 seizure (no relight)The same for engine 2.

The relight envelope comes from the LEAP-1A chart ENG RELIGHT IN FLIGHT of the Air Arabia A320 QRH (18-Aug-21, ABN-19.06A, A6-ATA to ATF, CFM LEAP-1A engines, the engines FBW models). It replaces the PW1100G chart of the IndiGo A320neo FCOM (PDF page 4761), used until 2026-10-05 while no LEAP-1A chart was available. Vc is the indicated airspeed, Zp the pressure altitude.

ZoneWhereNeeds

1, starter assisted relight135-270 kt up to 15 000 ft; 200-270 kt from 15 000 to 27 000 ftENG MODE IGN, ENG MASTER OFF then ON, and starter air (APU BLEED, or X BLEED OPEN with the other engine)

  • Above these limits (e.g. in cruise at FL 350) no relight lights up.
  • A windmilling engine (no combustion, in flight) keeps N2 = 8 % x IAS / 270 kt and N1 = 1.5 x N2 (at 300 kt: N2 8.9 %, N1 13.3 %); on the ground it runs down to 0. Its fuel flow shows 0 and its oil pressure falls with N2.
  • During an in-flight relight both igniters are supplied (A320 FCOM DSC-70-80-30), whatever the ENG MODE selector: the SD ENG page shows A B.
  • The SD ENG page reads the N2 the FADEC shows, so a seized core reads 0 there as on the E/WD.
  • ENG ALL ENGINES FAILURE gets its FCOM flight phase inhibition (phases 1-4 and 8-10). Its OPT RELIGHT SPD line reads 270/.77, the LEAP-1A value of the same QRH (ABN-19.01A ALL ENG FAIL; the CFM56 aircraft use 300/.77); the removal of the unused 280/300/260 lines is on core/fcom-audit-fixes.

Limitations and design choices

LimitationWhyWhat to do
The relight envelope applies to every in-flight relight, also after a crew shutdown without any failure.Design choice: MSFS would otherwise relight anywhere; the QRH chart is the limit of any relight.Relight inside the envelope (descend and adjust the speed).
The envelope corners are read off the QRH chart grid.The chart gives no table; the calibrated airspeed is taken as the indicated airspeed.-
The quick relight also needs N2 above 15 %.Design choice kept from the PW1100G chart (the LEAP-1A chart only says t < 20 s): the sign of an engine that was running before the MASTER cycle.An engine that has windmilled for a while needs ENG MODE IGN (zone 1 or 3).
The windmill N1 / N2 values are design values.The chart only marks zone 3 "N2 > 8 %"; N2 is taken proportional to the airspeed, 8 % at 270 kt, and N1 = 1.5 x N2.-
The seizure stops the core in about 3 s.Design choice: the FCOM gives no rate.-
ENG 1(2) FAIL does not come back after the ENG MASTER OFF, even if the relight does not light up.Design choice: the MASTER OFF disarms the alert; ENG SHUT DOWN is shown meanwhile.After a failed attempt (no light-up within 30 s), MASTER OFF and try again.
While ENG ALL ENGINES FAILURE is active, the single engine alerts are not shown.Design choice: its procedure replaces them.-
The HP fuel valve opening at N2 > 15 % is not modelled.The fuel comes back when the engine lights up inside the envelope.-
On this branch the oil pressure of a running engine is the stock FBW one.The FADEC oil model of develop is a fork-only change (47e00cee8) that is not on this base; only the shutdown oil pressure (following N2) comes with this feature.-
A full MSFS restart is needed after an update.The systems WASM module and the FADEC WASM module change.Restart the sim after installing.

How to use

  1. Open flyPad > Failures > ATA 72 Engine and activate a flameout or a seizure.
  2. Follow the ECAM (ENG FAIL, then ENG SHUT DOWN). To relight, get inside the envelope (table above), ENG MODE IGN, ENG MASTER OFF for 30 s, then ON. For a quick relight in zone 2, set the MASTER OFF then ON at once (within 20 s, N2 above 15 %, 230-270 kt up to 20 000 ft).
  3. Clear the failure in the flyPad after the test; a seizure must be cleared before a relight.

How to test

From the test plan reports/test-plan-engine-failures-a1.md. Setups: S1 cruise 60 t, FL 350, M.78; S2 60 t, 10 000 ft, 300 kt; S3 60 t, 15 000 ft, 210 kt, APU running with APU BLEED ON; S4 on the ground, both engines at idle.

2T1: after 30 s, ENG MASTER 1 OFF; then MASTER ON at FL 350.ENG 1 SHUT DOWN with its STATUS and INOP SYS; at FL 350 no light-up (outside the envelope), A B igniters on the SD ENG page.

5T4 (S2 slowed to 245 kt below 13 000 ft, ENG MODE NORM): flameout, then at once MASTER 1 OFF then ON within 20 s.Quick relight at NORM; the ENG MODE knob stays at NORM and still works afterwards. Waiting until N2 is below 15 % before the MASTER OFF / ON: no light-up.
6T5 (S2): activate Engine 2 seizure (no relight).N2 to 0 in about 3 s, N1 windmills, oil pressure 0, ENG 2 FAIL; MASTER 2 OFF 30 s / ON at 300 kt: no light-up. Clear the seizure, MASTER 2 OFF 30 s / ON: light-up.

With the FIRE pb: ENG 1 SHUT DOWN at once, no ENG 1 FAIL; the engine stops after about 100 s on its residual fuel (LP valve starvation of core/fire).
