1. Strobe double flash and beacon phase

Aircraft: A32NX and A380X  |  Where: EXT LT panel: STROBE ON, BEACON ON; outside view  |  Status: Checked in the sim by the user at night on both aircraft ("both look right", 2026-10-02).

What it is for

The FCOM gives no flash rates (A320 DSC-33-20-20, A380 DSC-33-30-20); the A380 FCOM only says that the strobes and the beacons alternate. The timing of the real lights comes from the Collins LED light data: a wing strobe double flash of 50 ms on, 60 ms off, 50 ms on, about 60 times a minute, the beacons flashing between the strobes. The FBW effects had a 50 ms gap (A32NX) and a 150 ms gap (A380X) in the double flash, and the A380X beacons flashed too close to the strobes.

How to use

  1. Nothing to set: switch the STROBE and BEACON lights ON on the overhead EXT LT panel as usual.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A32NX at night (or dusk), on the ground. Switch STROBE ON and BEACON ON. Go to an outside view that shows a wing tip and the fuselage.Each wing strobe gives a crisp double flash (two short flashes very close together) about once a second.
2Watch the beacon and the strobes together.The beacon flashes halfway between two strobe double flashes: strobe, beacon, strobe, beacon, evenly spaced.
3Repeat with the A380X.Same crisp double flash of the wing strobes, a little slower (45 per minute); the beacons (top and belly) flash exactly halfway between the strobes, not right after them.
