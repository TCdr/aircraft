1. Landing gear sensor and actuator failures listed, ROLLOUT removed

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Failures > ATA 32 Landing Gear  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-03). Unit tests: a380.spec.ts.

What it is for

The landing gear proximity sensor and jammed actuator failures (ids 32004 to 32025) were already simulated by the common FBW gear system and mapped in the A380X systems, but the flyPad listed only one of them, and with the wrong name: "uplock nose gear #1" failed the right main gear uplock sensor #1. All 18 failures are now listed, with the ids and names of the Rust failure map (the same as on the A32NX).

The ROLLOUT failure (ATA 22) only set an FCDC input that nothing reads (there is no steering control system yet). It is no longer listed; its id stays in the code so the C++ failure list keeps in step.

3. Brake and trim air failures

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Failures > ATA 32 and ATA 21  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-04); the brake leak works but shows nothing on the SD HYD page.

What it is for

The A380X hydraulic and air conditioning models already had brake leak and trim air faults, but the flyPad had no failure for them. Eleven failures are now listed (same ids as on the A32NX for the brakes), and a spec checks that every id of the systems failure map is listed with its label.

flyPad name (ATA)What it fails
Brakes NORM circuit leak (green hydraulic) (32)The NORM brakes are fed by the GREEN system in this model: GREEN fluid leaks out (0.1 gal/s) while the circuit is pressurised.
Brakes ALTN circuit leak (yellow hydraulic) (32)The ALTN brakes (with the brake accumulator) are fed by the YELLOW system: YELLOW fluid leaks out while pressurised.
Brakes ALTN accumulator gas leak (32)The ALTN brake accumulator loses its gas charge: the stored brake pressure drops when the YELLOW system is not pressurised.
Cockpit / Main deck zone 1 / Upper deck zone 1 / Forward cargo trim air valve jammed (21)The trim air valve of that zone stays where it was when the failure was set.
Cockpit / Main deck zone 1 / Upper deck zone 1 / Forward cargo duct overheat (hot trim air) (21)200 deg C air is blown into the zone duct while its hot air valve is open. The zone gets too hot and the controller closes the trim air valves; HOT AIR OFF stops the overheat (A380 FCOM COND DUCT OVHT procedure).

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
No ECAM alert and no SD indication for the brake failures.The SD HYD page has no brake leak indication. The trim air failures now have their alerts and SD marks: see "Trim air duct overheat and jammed valve alerts" below.Watch the hydraulic quantity / pressure and the brake pressure.
A full MSFS restart is needed after an update.The systems WASM module changes.Restart the sim after installing.

How to use

  1. Brakes: flyPad > Failures > ATA 32 Landing Gear, the three "Brakes ..." entries.
  2. Trim air: flyPad > Failures > ATA 21 Air Conditioning, the "... trim air valve jammed" and "... duct overheat (hot trim air)" entries.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A380X on the ground, four engines running (GREEN and YELLOW pressurised). Activate Brakes NORM circuit leak (green hydraulic).The GREEN system loses fluid; YELLOW is not affected. Nothing is shown for the leak itself on the SD HYD page.
2Deactivate it, activate Brakes ALTN circuit leak (yellow hydraulic).The YELLOW system loses fluid; GREEN is not affected.
3Set the parking brake, then stop the engines (YELLOW no longer pressurised). Activate Brakes ALTN accumulator gas leak.The brake pressure held by the accumulator drops.
4In flight, HOT AIR pushbuttons ON, activate Cockpit duct overheat (hot trim air). Open the SD COND page.The cockpit zone gets hotter; then the trim air valves close. Setting HOT AIR OFF stops the overheat.
5Activate Cockpit trim air valve jammed, then change the cockpit temperature selection.The cockpit trim air valve no longer moves: the cockpit temperature stops following the selection; the other zones still regulate.

4. Trim air duct overheat and jammed valve alerts

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: overhead AIR panel (HOT AIR 1 / 2, PACK 1 / 2); E/WD, STATUS; SD COND page  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-04). Rust tests (air_conditioning), unit tests: CondTrimAirFlags.spec.ts, CondTrimAirMarks.spec.ts.

What it is for

The trim air failures above now give the alerts of the A380 FCOM (PRO-ABN-ECAM-10-21-10). The temperature controller monitors each trim air duct and valve: a duct above 70 deg C for 1.2 s is an overheat, latched until its HOT AIR pushbutton is set OFF; a valve that does not follow its demand is jammed. The FWS reads both from the controller and raises:

  • COND DUCT OVHT (master caution, single chime, FAULT light in the HOT AIR pushbutton, SD COND page) with the CKPT DUCT OVHT, CABIN DUCT OVHT and FWD CARGO DUCT OVHT cases, the deferred procedure WHEN DUCT OVHT OUT, and its STATUS lines (INOP SYS, CABIN TEMP REGUL DEGRADED, FWD CRG TEMP REGUL DEGRADED).
  • COND FWD CARGO TEMP REGUL FAULT for a jammed forward cargo trim air valve: CARGO TRIM AIR VLV FAULT, CARGO TEMP ... MONITOR, STATUS INFO FWD CRG TEMP REGUL DEGRADED (SD COND page only: no chime, no master light).
  • SD COND page: the amber OVHT mark of an overheated cockpit or cabin zone duct and the trim air valve mark of a jammed valve (before: never shown).

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
Cockpit duct overheatCOND DUCT OVHT, CKPT DUCT OVHT: HOT AIR 2 ... OFF; if the hot air valve stays open (HOT AIR JAMMED OPEN): PACK 2 ... OFF. HOT AIR 2 FAULT light, OVHT on the SD COND page.
Main deck zone duct overheatCABIN DUCT OVHT: HOT AIR 1 ... OFF (HOT AIR 2 for an upper deck zone); if jammed open, PACK 1 (2) ... OFF; CARGO TEMP ... MONITOR.
Forward cargo duct overheatFWD CARGO DUCT OVHT: HOT AIR 1 ... OFF; if jammed open, PACK 1 ... OFF.
Overheat gone (duct cooled, HOT AIR OFF)Deferred procedure WHEN DUCT OVHT OUT: HOT AIR 1 (2) ... ON, and PACK 1 (2) ... ON if it was switched off.
Forward cargo trim air valve jammedCOND FWD CARGO TEMP REGUL FAULT; STATUS INFO FWD CRG TEMP REGUL DEGRADED; the valve mark on the SD COND page.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
Which HOT AIR valve supplies which zone is a design choice.The FCOM does not say it: the main deck zones and the forward cargo are on HOT AIR 1, the cockpit and the upper deck on HOT AIR 2.-
A jammed forward cargo valve counts as a degraded regulation (no INOP SYS).Design choice: the aircraft has two forward cargo trim air valves and the FCOM says a jammed valve loses the "optimized" regulation.-
The alerts are inhibited during the takeoff and the landing (FWS phases 3 to 7, 9, 10).The FCOM gives the inhibition as a figure: that of COND HOT AIR FAULT / FWD CARGO ISOL FAULT is used.Test on the ground before takeoff or in cruise.
A full MSFS restart is needed after an update.The systems WASM module changes.Restart the sim after installing.

How to use

  1. Open flyPad > Failures > ATA 21 Air Conditioning and activate a "... duct overheat (hot trim air)" or a "... trim air valve jammed" failure.
  2. Follow the ECAM with the HOT AIR and PACK pushbuttons of the overhead AIR panel; check the SD COND page.
  3. Deactivate the failure; when the duct has cooled, follow WHEN DUCT OVHT OUT.

5. Feed tank pump and crossfeed valve failures

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Failures > ATA 28 Fuel; overhead FUEL panel; SD FUEL  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-04), after the fix that holds the MSFS pump / valve switch.

What it is for

Twelve new failures: the main and standby pump of each feed tank and the four crossfeed valves. The FUEL panel pushbuttons now set a selection, and the systems host moves the MSFS pump or valve switch from it: a failed pump is held off, a jammed valve is held where it was. The FAULT lights, the SD FUEL page and the FCOM alerts compare the selection with the real state (A380 FCOM PRO-ABN-ECAM-10-28, DSC-28-20).

6. Transfer pump failures and fuel follow-ups

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Failures > ATA 28 Fuel; overhead FUEL panel; E/WD, STATUS; SD FUEL page  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-04). Unit tests: FuelPumpValveFlags.spec.ts, FuelPumpsAndValves.spec.ts, failable-fuel-switches.spec.ts.

What it is for

Eight new failures of the transfer pumps that the FBW fuel transfers use: L outer tank pump, R outer tank pump, L mid tank fwd pump, R mid tank fwd pump, L inner tank fwd pump, R inner tank fwd pump, Trim tank L pump, Trim tank R pump. A failed pump is held off whatever the fuel system commands. The pushbutton FAULT light, the amber pump on the SD FUEL page and the FUEL ... PMP FAULT alerts of the A380 FCOM (PRO-ABN-ECAM-10-28) come as soon as a pump fails, runs in an empty tank with its pushbutton ON, or is switched OFF by the crew (except in flight phases 1 and 12). A repaired pump follows the automatic transfers again.

  • FUEL FEED TK 1(2)(3)(4) LEVEL LO now triggers for a single low feed tank: the four tanks shared one 30 s confirmation, so only all four low together could trigger it.
  • The CROSSFEED pushbutton OPEN light now follows its valve.
  • The sensed CROSSFEED ... ON and ALL FEED TKs PMPs ON items of the ECAM procedures tick on the pushbuttons, even with a jammed valve or a failed pump.
  • Corrupted degree signs in the ECAM texts are fixed.
