1. Landing gear sensor and actuator failures listed, ROLLOUT removed

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Failures > ATA 32 Landing Gear  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-03). Unit tests: a380.spec.ts.

What it is for

The landing gear proximity sensor and jammed actuator failures (ids 32004 to 32025) were already simulated by the common FBW gear system and mapped in the A380X systems, but the flyPad listed only one of them, and with the wrong name: "uplock nose gear #1" failed the right main gear uplock sensor #1. All 18 failures are now listed, with the ids and names of the Rust failure map (the same as on the A32NX).

The ROLLOUT failure (ATA 22) only set an FCDC input that nothing reads (there is no steering control system yet). It is no longer listed; its id stays in the code so the C++ failure list keeps in step.

NoteA door uplock sensor ("closed ... door") detects the door closed, a door downlock sensor ("opened ... door") the door open. The 32021 / 32022 and 32024 / 32025 names come from the clearer names feature below: one failure jams the whole left or right main gear group.

How to use

  1. Open flyPad > Failures > ATA 32 Landing Gear.
  2. Pick the sensor or actuator failure by its name (the table above gives what each one fails) and activate it.
  3. Deactivate it to clear the failure.

3. Brake and trim air failures

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Failures > ATA 32 and ATA 21  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-04); the brake leak works but shows nothing on the SD HYD page.

What it is for

The A380X hydraulic and air conditioning models already had brake leak and trim air faults, but the flyPad had no failure for them. Eleven failures are now listed (same ids as on the A32NX for the brakes), and a spec checks that every id of the systems failure map is listed with its label.

flyPad name (ATA)What it fails
Brakes NORM circuit leak (green hydraulic) (32)The NORM brakes are fed by the GREEN system in this model: GREEN fluid leaks out (0.1 gal/s) while the circuit is pressurised.
Brakes ALTN circuit leak (yellow hydraulic) (32)The ALTN brakes (with the brake accumulator) are fed by the YELLOW system: YELLOW fluid leaks out while pressurised.
Brakes ALTN accumulator gas leak (32)The ALTN brake accumulator loses its gas charge: the stored brake pressure drops when the YELLOW system is not pressurised.
Cockpit / Main deck zone 1 / Upper deck zone 1 / Forward cargo trim air valve jammed (21)The trim air valve of that zone stays where it was when the failure was set.
Cockpit / Main deck zone 1 / Upper deck zone 1 / Forward cargo duct overheat (hot trim air) (21)200 deg C air is blown into the zone duct while its hot air valve is open. The zone gets too hot and the controller closes the trim air valves; HOT AIR OFF stops the overheat (A380 FCOM COND DUCT OVHT procedure).

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
No ECAM alert and no SD indication for these failures.The COND DUCT OVHT alert is defined but not raised on this branch, the duct overheat / trim air marks of the SD COND page are not wired, and the SD HYD page has no brake leak indication.Watch the effect: zone temperatures on the SD COND page, hydraulic quantity / pressure, brake pressure.
A full MSFS restart is needed after an update.The systems WASM module changes.Restart the sim after installing.

How to use

  1. Brakes: flyPad > Failures > ATA 32 Landing Gear, the three "Brakes ..." entries.
  2. Trim air: flyPad > Failures > ATA 21 Air Conditioning, the "... trim air valve jammed" and "... duct overheat (hot trim air)" entries.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A380X on the ground, four engines running (GREEN and YELLOW pressurised). Activate Brakes NORM circuit leak (green hydraulic).The GREEN system loses fluid; YELLOW is not affected. Nothing is shown for the leak itself on the SD HYD page.
2Deactivate it, activate Brakes ALTN circuit leak (yellow hydraulic).The YELLOW system loses fluid; GREEN is not affected.
3Set the parking brake, then stop the engines (YELLOW no longer pressurised). Activate Brakes ALTN accumulator gas leak.The brake pressure held by the accumulator drops.
4In flight, HOT AIR pushbuttons ON, activate Cockpit duct overheat (hot trim air). Open the SD COND page.The cockpit zone gets hotter; then the trim air valves close. Setting HOT AIR OFF stops the overheat.
5Activate Cockpit trim air valve jammed, then change the cockpit temperature selection.The cockpit trim air valve no longer moves: the cockpit temperature stops following the selection; the other zones still regulate.

4. Feed tank pump and crossfeed valve failures

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Failures > ATA 28 Fuel; overhead FUEL panel; SD FUEL  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-04), after the fix that holds the MSFS pump / valve switch.

What it is for

Twelve new failures: the main and standby pump of each feed tank and the four crossfeed valves. The FUEL panel pushbuttons now set a selection, and the systems host moves the MSFS pump or valve switch from it: a failed pump is held off, a jammed valve is held where it was. The FAULT lights, the SD FUEL page and the FCOM alerts compare the selection with the real state (A380 FCOM PRO-ABN-ECAM-10-28, DSC-28-20).

flyPad name (ATA 28)What it fails
Feed tank 1 main pump ... Feed tank 4 main pumpThat main pump stops (held off).
Feed tank 1 standby pump ... Feed tank 4 standby pumpThat standby pump stops (held off).
Crossfeed valve 1 jammed in place ... Crossfeed valve 4 jammed in placeThat crossfeed valve stays where it was.

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
Feed pump failed, its pushbutton ONFAULT light in the pushbutton; the pump amber on the SD FUEL page.
One main pump failedFUEL FEED TK 1 MAIN PMP FAULT (tank 1 to 4), SD FUEL page; procedure FEED TK 1 MAIN PMP ... OFF; STATUS INOP SYS FEED TK 1 MAIN PMP.
One standby pump failedFUEL FEED TK 1 STBY PMP FAULT, crew awareness; STATUS MORE: REDUND LOSS FEED TK 1 STBY PMP.
Main and standby pumps of one tank failedFUEL FEED TK 1 MAIN + STBY PMPs FAULT (replaces the two single alerts). After takeoff: CROSSFEED 1 ON, CROSSFEED 2 ON (CROSSFEED 3 and 4 for tanks 3 and 4), FEED TK 1 MAIN PMP OFF, FEED TK 1 STBY PMP OFF, AVOID NEG G LOAD; before takeoff only the two pump lines. STATUS: INOP SYS FEED TK 1 PMPs, FEED TK 1 BY GRVTY ONLY.
Crossfeed valve jammed and its pushbutton movedThe valve disagrees with its pushbutton: FAULT light, valve amber on the SD FUEL page, FUEL CROSSFEED VLV 1 FAULT (crew awareness), STATUS INOP SYS CROSSFEED VLV 1; if the valve is stuck closed and engine 1 is not running, FEED TK 1 NOT USABLE.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
The alerts are inhibited during the takeoff and the landing.The FCOM gives the inhibition as a figure: the one of JETTISON VLV NOT CLOSED is used (FWS flight phases 4 to 7, 9 and 10).Test on the ground before takeoff or in cruise.
A pump turned OFF by the crew also gives the FAULT alert.FCOM: a pump is faulty when it runs at low pressure, is abnormally not running, or is turned off by the crew (except in flight phases 1 and 12).Expected behaviour.
The gravity feed ceiling (MAX FL 280, 50/MEA) is not simulated.No gravity feed model.None.
A full MSFS restart is needed after an update.The cockpit behaviour XML changes.Restart the sim after installing.

How to use

  1. Open flyPad > Failures > ATA 28 Fuel and activate a pump or crossfeed valve failure.
  2. Follow the ECAM procedure with the overhead FUEL panel pushbuttons; check the SD FUEL page.
  3. Deactivate the failure to restore the pump or valve.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A380X on the ground, engines running, all feed pumps ON, crossfeed valves in their normal position. Open the SD FUEL page.No FAULT light, no fuel alert.
2Activate Feed tank 1 main pump.FAULT light in the FEED TK 1 MAIN pushbutton, pump amber on the SD, E/WD: FUEL FEED TK 1 MAIN PMP FAULT with FEED TK 1 MAIN PMP ... OFF.
3Set the FEED TK 1 MAIN pushbutton OFF. Open the STATUS page.The procedure line is done; STATUS INOP SYS: FEED TK 1 MAIN PMP.
4Set it ON again and also activate Feed tank 1 standby pump.The single alerts are replaced by FUEL FEED TK 1 MAIN + STBY PMPs FAULT (on the ground: the two pump OFF lines); STATUS: FEED TK 1 PMPs, FEED TK 1 BY GRVTY ONLY.
5Deactivate both. Activate Feed tank 2 standby pump only.FUEL FEED TK 2 STBY PMP FAULT; STATUS MORE: REDUND LOSS FEED TK 2 STBY PMP.
6Deactivate it. Activate Crossfeed valve 1 jammed in place, then move the CROSSFEED 1 pushbutton.The valve does not move: FAULT light, valve amber on the SD, FUEL CROSSFEED VLV 1 FAULT, STATUS INOP SYS CROSSFEED VLV 1.
7Deactivate the failure.The valve follows its pushbutton again; the alert clears.
