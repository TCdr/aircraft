1. A32NX ND and ECAM display unit failures

Aircraft: A32NX  |  Where: flyPad > Failures > ATA 31; CPT / F/O ND, upper and lower ECAM  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-04).

What it is for

The flyPad could fail the two PFDs only. Four more display units (DUs) can now be failed: the CAPT and F/O NDs and the upper and lower ECAM DUs. A failed DU goes blank, the way a failed PFD already did. The A320 FCOM (DSC-31-05-60, FAILURE OF A DU) lists a blank screen with an amber F, a distorted display or a blank screen with INVALID DISPLAY UNIT; the blank (OFF) screen is used here.

  • The weather radar and terrain picture of the ND (the native gauge over it) goes blank with a failed ND.
  • It is also no longer drawn on an ND that is switched off (brightness knob OFF) or still in its self-test.
  • The lower ECAM failure blanks the whole SD (both of its display layers). Before, the SD display unit ignored a failure change.
  • When the failure is cleared, the DU runs its self-test, then shows its picture again.

The flyPad failures (ATA 31 Indicating / Recording System)

flyPad failure nameDisplay unit that goes blank
Captain PFD displayCAPT PFD (existing failure)
F/O PFD displayF/O PFD (existing failure)
Captain ND displayCAPT ND, with its weather / terrain picture
F/O ND displayF/O ND, with its weather / terrain picture
Upper ECAM displayUpper ECAM DU (E/WD)
Lower ECAM displayLower ECAM DU (SD)

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
No ECAM alert for a failed DU.The branch only blanks the DU.-
No automatic transfer of a picture to another DU.The FCOM transfers (E/WD to the lower ECAM DU, PFD to the ND DU, DSC-31-05-60) are not part of this branch.Use the ECAM / EIS switching as available.
Always a blank screen.The amber F, the distorted picture and INVALID DISPLAY UNIT of the FCOM are not drawn.-

How to use

  1. Open flyPad > Failures, chapter ATA 31 Indicating / Recording System.
  2. Activate one of the display failures (table above): that DU goes blank.
  3. Deactivate it to get the DU back (after its self-test).

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A32NX, any phase, all displays on. CPT ND in ARC with the weather radar on (weather around) or TERR ON ND. flyPad > Failures > ATA 31: activate Captain ND display.The CPT ND goes blank, with no weather or terrain left on it. The F/O ND is not affected.

2. A380X CDS display unit failures

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Failures > ATA 31; the 8 CDS DUs; E/WD; SD STATUS  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-04).

What it is for

The eight display units of the A380 CDS (A380 FCOM DSC-31-15-10: CAPT PFD, CAPT ND, CAPT MFD, E/WD, SD, F/O MFD, F/O ND, F/O PFD) can be failed from the flyPad. The failed DU goes blank (on an ND, the weather radar and terrain picture too, the VD included) and the FWS raises the CDS ... DU FAULT alert with its FCOM procedure and its INOP SYS line (FCOM PRO-ABN-ECAM-10-31).

The flyPad failures (ATA 31 Indicating / Recording System)

CAPT ND display unitCDS CAPT ND DU FAULTCAPT ND DU

EWD display unitCDS EWD DU FAULTEWD DU
SD display unitCDS SD DU FAULT(none: the FCOM lists no SD line)
F/O MFD display unitCDS F/O MFD DU FAULTF/O MFD DU

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
Failure activatedThe DU goes blank. Single chime, MASTER CAUT, and the amber alert on the E/WD.
ECAM procedure (each DU)xx DU ..... OFF 5 S THEN ON; IF NOT SUCCESSFUL: xx DU ..... OFF; DU RECONF P/B AVAIL (green). The SD DU FAULT adds: IF SD DU OFF : F/O DU RECONF P/B FOR ATC MAILBOX.
SD STATUS pageINOP SYS (all phases): the DU line of the table above.
Takeoff and landingThe alert is inhibited in the FWS flight phases 3 to 7, 9 and 10 (FCOM PDF p. 5386); the DU is blank anyway.
DU without powerNo DU FAULT alert (design choice): the lost busbar has its own ELEC alert.
Failure clearedThe DU runs its self-test and shows its picture again; the alert and the INOP SYS line go.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
No automatic reconfiguration (PFD to the ND DU, E/WD to the SD DU), no DU RECONF pushbutton.Not modelled on this branch.With the EWD or SD DU failed, read the alert or the STATUS on the other ECAM DU when it is not the failed one.
The OFF 5 S THEN ON item does not bring the DU back.The flyPad failure stays until it is cleared, which is the procedure's NOT SUCCESSFUL branch.Clear the failure in the flyPad.
The second FCOM trigger (a DU that declared two other DUs faulty in turn) is not modelled.It needs the DU monitoring loops, which are not simulated.-

How to use

  1. Open flyPad > Failures, chapter ATA 31 Indicating / Recording System.
  2. Activate one of the eight ... display unit failures.
  3. Follow the ECAM procedure, then check the SD STATUS page.
  4. Deactivate the failure to get the DU back.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A380X on the ground (before the takeoff power) or in cruise, all DUs on. CPT ND in ARC with the WX overlay and weather around. flyPad > Failures > ATA 31: activate CAPT ND display unit.The CAPT ND and its VD go blank, with no weather or terrain left. Single chime, MASTER CAUT. E/WD: CDS CAPT ND DU FAULT with CAPT ND DU ..... OFF 5 S THEN ON, IF NOT SUCCESSFUL, CAPT ND DU ..... OFF, DU RECONF P/B AVAIL in green.
2Open the SD STATUS page.INOP SYS: CAPT ND DU.
3Deactivate the failure.The CAPT ND runs its self-test, then shows the ND, the VD and the weather again; the alert and the INOP SYS line are gone.
4Repeat with CAPT PFD display unit, CAPT MFD display unit, F/O PFD display unit, F/O ND display unit and F/O MFD display unit.Each time the named DU goes blank and the E/WD shows the matching CDS ... DU FAULT, with its INOP SYS line on the STATUS.
5Activate SD display unit.The SD goes blank; E/WD: CDS SD DU FAULT with the extra green line IF SD DU OFF : F/O DU RECONF P/B FOR ATC MAILBOX (the FCOM gives this alert no INOP SYS line).
6Deactivate it; activate EWD display unit.The E/WD goes blank (its alert cannot be read: no reconfiguration); single chime and MASTER CAUT; the SD STATUS page lists INOP SYS EWD DU.
7Activate any DU failure during the takeoff roll (above takeoff power).The DU goes blank but no alert until the end of the inhibition.
