1. A32NX ND and ECAM display unit failures

Aircraft: A32NX  |  Where: flyPad > Failures > ATA 31; CPT / F/O ND, upper and lower ECAM  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-04).

What it is for

The flyPad could fail the two PFDs only. Four more display units (DUs) can now be failed: the CAPT and F/O NDs and the upper and lower ECAM DUs. A failed DU goes blank, the way a failed PFD already did. The A320 FCOM (DSC-31-05-60, FAILURE OF A DU) lists a blank screen with an amber F, a distorted display or a blank screen with INVALID DISPLAY UNIT; the blank (OFF) screen is used here.

  • The weather radar and terrain picture of the ND (the native gauge over it) goes blank with a failed ND.
  • It is also no longer drawn on an ND that is switched off (brightness knob OFF) or still in its self-test.
  • The lower ECAM failure blanks the whole SD (both of its display layers). Before, the SD display unit ignored a failure change.
  • When the failure is cleared, the DU runs its self-test, then shows its picture again.

The flyPad failures (ATA 31 Indicating / Recording System)

flyPad failure nameDisplay unit that goes blank

F/O PFD displayF/O PFD (existing failure)
Captain ND displayCAPT ND, with its weather / terrain picture
F/O ND displayF/O ND, with its weather / terrain picture

How to use

  1. Open flyPad > Failures, chapter ATA 31 Indicating / Recording System.
  2. Activate one of the display failures (table above): that DU goes blank.
  3. Deactivate it to get the DU back (after its self-test).

2. A32NX display unit reconfiguration

Aircraft: A32NX  |  Where: PFD/ND XFR pbs; PFD and ND brightness knobs; SWITCHING panel ECAM/ND XFR; ECAM control panel page keys  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user on all steps: OK (2026-10-05). Unit tests: DisplayReconfiguration.spec.ts (shared), DmcDisplayReconfiguration.spec.ts (systems host), displayUnit.spec.tsx (common DU and legacy SD).

What it is for

The A320 moves the picture of a lost display unit (DU) to another one (A320 FCOM DSC-31-05-60 RECONFIGURING DUS). Before, a failed or switched-off A32NX DU simply lost its picture. Now the PFD, the ND, the E/WD and the SD can be shown on another DU, automatically or with the cockpit controls, as the FCOM describes.

NoteA320 FCOM DSC-31-05-60 (a320_fcom.txt l.45264, l.45304): if the upper ECAM display fails or is switched off, "the engine/warning page automatically replaces the system/status page on the lower ECAM DU"; "If a PFDU fails, the system automatically transfers the PFD image to the NDU." (l.45259-45325)
NoteA320 FCOM DSC-31-50 (l.49317-49332): turning the PFD brightness knob fully counterclockwise switches off the PFD, its image goes to the NDU, and the pilot can recover the ND with the PFD/ND XFR pushbutton. DSC-31-30 (l.46861-46867): the ECAM/ND XFR selector moves the system/status display to the CAPT or F/O ND with "ECAM ON ND" on the lower ECAM display; with both ECAM DUs failed it moves the E/WD, without that message.

How it is done

No extra copies of the instruments. MSFS draws a gauge on the model material of the same name, so every DU has one glass per picture it can show (17 overlay glasses carrying the material of that picture, an ECAM ON ND glass and a blank glass). The DMC logic of the systems host writes the picture of each DU, and the cockpit behaviour shows the matching glass. The brightness stays the DU's own knob. A new small gauge (DuMessage) draws the ECAM ON ND message.

Design choices

  • The DU that receives a picture does not run a self-test: the picture is already live (the FCOM gives none).
  • A system page key must be held 0.5 s before the SD appears: a click that only selects or deselects a page does not flash the SD, and the SD has its new page drawn before it is shown.
  • ECAM ON ND is drawn in the style of the SELF TEST IN PROGRESS message: the FCOM gives no colour.
  • A lost DU shows dark glass (a blank screen, which the FCOM allows for a failed DU).
  • An unpowered DU counts as unavailable, like a failed one or one switched off with its knob, so its picture moves.
  • A change of the PFD DU (lost or back) restarts from the automatic configuration: the PFD always goes to an available DU first.

How to use

  1. Nothing to do for the automatic transfers: a lost PFD DU or upper ECAM DU moves its picture by itself.
  2. Press the PFD/ND XFR pushbutton of your side to swap your PFD and ND (or to get the ND back after an automatic PFD transfer).
  3. To see the SD with an ECAM DU lost: turn the ECAM/ND XFR selector on the SWITCHING panel to CAPT or F/O, or hold the system page key on the ECAM control panel.
  4. Turn the ECAM/ND XFR selector back to NORM to get the ND back.

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: flyPad > Failures > ATA 31; the 8 CDS DUs; E/WD; SD STATUS  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-04).

What it is for

The eight display units of the A380 CDS (A380 FCOM DSC-31-15-10: CAPT PFD, CAPT ND, CAPT MFD, E/WD, SD, F/O MFD, F/O ND, F/O PFD) can be failed from the flyPad. The failed DU goes blank (on an ND, the weather radar and terrain picture too, the VD included) and the FWS raises the CDS ... DU FAULT alert with its FCOM procedure and its INOP SYS line (FCOM PRO-ABN-ECAM-10-31).

How to use

  1. Open flyPad > Failures, chapter ATA 31 Indicating / Recording System.
  2. Activate one of the eight ... display unit failures.
  3. Follow the ECAM procedure, then check the SD STATUS page.
  4. Deactivate the failure to get the DU back.

4. A380X display unit reconfiguration

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: CAPT and F/O PFD/ND and DU RECONF pushbuttons; the 8 CDS DUs  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user on all steps: OK (2026-10-04). Unit tests: CdsReconfiguration.spec.ts (shared and systems host), HostedDisplay.spec.ts, hostedInstrument.spec.ts; native ndwxr test cds_display_test.

What it is for

The A380 CDS moves a lost display to another display unit (A380 FCOM DSC-31-15-20 CDS abnormal operations, DSC-31-20-50 PFD/ND pb). Before, a failed or switched-off DU simply lost its picture. Now:

  • Automatic: when a PFD DU fails, loses its power or is switched off, the PFD is shown on the ND DU of the same side (the ND is lost). When the EWD DU is lost, the E/WD is shown on the SD DU.
  • PFD/ND pushbutton: exchanges the on-side PFD and ND. With the exchange selected, the PFD is on the ND DU and comes back to the PFD DU if the ND DU fails (the PFD keeps the priority).
  • DU RECONF pushbutton: active only with a failed on-side DU; it acts on the DU the FCOM gives (CAPT: the MFD DU, then the ND DU if the MFD DU is failed, then the PFD DU if the ND and MFD DUs are failed; F/O: the ND DU, the MFD DU if the PFD or ND DU is failed, then the PFD DU) and steps through the displays of the FCOM order that this DU can draw, then back to its normal display.
  • EWD and SD on the ND DU: with the CAPT (F/O) PFD and MFD DUs failed, the pb acts on the ND DU, which shows the PFD automatically; it now cycles PFD > EWD > SD > PFD (the MFD of the FCOM sequence cannot be drawn there). With only the MFD DU (CAPT) or the SD DU (F/O) failed, the ND DU cycles ND > EWD > SD > ND the same way. The EWD (SD) is never shown on both sides at once (A380 FCOM DSC-31-15-20), and a PFD that moves to the ND DU later (its PFD DU fails afterwards) wins over a selection there.
  • The weather radar and terrain picture (ndwxr) is hidden while its ND DU shows another display (the PFD, the EWD or the SD).

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
CAPT PFD display unit failed (or switched off)The CAPT PFD DU is blank and the PFD appears on the CAPT ND DU, without weather or terrain on it.
EWD display unit failedThe E/WD appears on the SD DU.
PFD/ND pb pressed (CAPT or F/O)The PFD and the ND of that side swap DUs; press again to swap back.

SD DU failed, F/O DU RECONF pressedThe F/O ND DU steps from the ND to the E/WD, then to the SD pages (without the ATC mailbox), then back to the ND.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
The MFD is never drawn on another DU, and nothing is drawn on the MFD DUs; the DU RECONF pb skips them, so it does nothing when it acts on an MFD DU.Every display is a gauge of its own on its DU texture: the MFD is one gauge over both MFD screens (one texture) that also runs the FMS. Design choice: the PFD and the ND on both DUs of a side, the E/WD and the SD also on the ND DUs, and the E/WD on the SD DU.Use the other side's MFD.
The SD on an ND DU has no ATC mailbox.The mailbox stays with the SD DU's own SD (design choice of the hosted SD).With the SD DU failed, the ATC mailbox is lost.
A selected E/WD or SD replaces the PFD on that side.With the PFD and MFD DUs of a side failed, the ND DU is the only DU left for the PFD (FCOM: the pb acts on the ND DU).Press DU RECONF again to get the PFD back.
The ND on the PFD DU has no weather radar or terrain image.The native ndwxr gauges stay on the ND DUs.Use the other side's ND.
The KCCU keys do not reconfigure the DUs.Not modelled.Use the DU RECONF pb.
A full MSFS restart is needed after an update.panel.cfg (new hosted gauges, the E/WD and the SD on the ND DUs included) and the cockpit behaviour XML change.Restart the sim after installing.

How to use

  1. Nothing to do for the automatic reconfiguration: a lost PFD or EWD DU moves its display by itself.
  2. Press the PFD/ND pushbutton of your side to exchange your PFD and ND.
  3. With a failed on-side DU, press DU RECONF to step the display of the DU it acts on.
  4. With your PFD and MFD DUs failed, press DU RECONF to bring the E/WD, then the SD, onto your ND DU; once more for the PFD.
