1. A32NX fire warnings only from a detected fire or the FIRE TEST

Aircraft: A32NX  |  Where: E/WD, master warning, overhead FIRE panel  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-03), with an engine fire set through SimConnect.

What it is for

The ENG 1 FIRE, ENG 2 FIRE and APU FIRE warnings were raised by the FIRE TEST or by the FIRE pushbutton. Releasing (pushing) a FIRE pushbutton without any fire showed the fire warning: a regression of the FBW E/WD port. On the aircraft the fire warning comes from the fire detection or the FIRE TEST only (A320 FCOM DSC-26-20-10). The warnings now use the sim fire signals that the red FIRE pushbutton lights already read (ENG ON FIRE:1/2, APU ON FIRE DETECTED).

  • The fire chime is a normal red warning chime: MASTER WARN cancels it (FCOM DSC-31-10), and pushing the FIRE pushbutton silences it (FCOM DSC-26-20-20).
  • The forced master warning and chime of the FIRE TEST pushbuttons are unchanged.
  • The APU AGENT AFTER 10 S countdown now starts with the APU FIRE pushbutton (FCOM PRO-ABN-APUF). Before, it started from the ENG 2 FIRE pushbutton and the ENG 1 AGENT 1 pushbutton (a copy-paste error).

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
ENG or APU FIRE pushbutton pushed, no fireNo fire warning, no chime (before: ENG / APU FIRE warning).
Engine fire detectedENG 1(2) FIRE warning, master warning, continuous chime, red FIRE pushbutton light.
MASTER WARN pressed during the fireThe chime stops; the warning stays on the E/WD.
FIRE pushbutton pushed during the fireThe chime stops (the pushbutton silences the aural).
APU fire, APU FIRE pushbutton pushedThe APU AGENT AFTER 10 S line counts down from the APU FIRE pushbutton.
FIRE TEST pushbutton heldSame as before: test warnings, master warning, chime, lights.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
A fire set in the sim stays on while the engine runs.MSFS keeps ENG ON FIRE at 1 while the engine turns and ignores the extinguish event; FBW AGENT pushbuttons do not send it.Stop the engine (ENG MASTER OFF, or the LP valve below) to end the fire.
There is no flyPad fire failure on this branch.The fire detection model and the fire failures are on core/failures-a32nx.Use that branch, or set the sim variable with a SimConnect tool.

How to use

  1. Nothing to set: the warnings follow the fire detection.
  2. In a fire, follow the ECAM: MASTER WARN to silence, then the ENG FIRE procedure (ENG MASTER OFF, FIRE pushbutton PUSH, AGENT 1 after 10 s...).

2. A32NX: the ENG FIRE pushbutton closes the LP fuel valve

Aircraft: A32NX  |  Where: Overhead ENG FIRE pushbuttons; SD FUEL page  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-03): the engine starved, then restarted with IGN/START.

What it is for

A32NX FCOM DSC-28-10-30: "The LP fuel valve is closed by either the engine master switch or the ENG FIRE PUSH pushbutton". Nothing linked the FIRE pushbutton to a fuel valve, so an engine kept running for ever with its FIRE pushbutton pushed. Each engine now has its LP valve: closed by the ENG MASTER switch OFF or the ENG FIRE pushbutton, with the 1.7 s travel of the engine valves.

Once the valve is closed, the fuel left between the valve and the engine is burned at the engine fuel flow. The FCOM ENG FIRE procedure says the engine stops "UP TO 2 MIN 30 S" after the LP valve closes, when that fuel is burned (PRO-ABN-ENG). The amount is sized on the lowest ground idle fuel flow (14 100 ft ISA), so the engine stops after about 99 s at a sea-level idle, sooner at higher thrust. The fuel is refilled when the valve opens again.

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
ENG FIRE pushbutton pushed, ENG MASTER still ONSD FUEL: the LP valve symbol closes (crossline). The engine keeps running on the remaining fuel, then shuts down (about 1 min 40 s at sea-level idle, never more than 2 min 30 s).
ENG MASTER OFFThe LP valve closes too (the engine also shuts down through its HP valve, as before).
Engine starvedThe FADEC handles it as a closed fuel valve: shutdown, no relight while starved.
FIRE pushbutton reset and ENG MASTER ON againThe LP valve opens, the line refills, the engine can be restarted with the normal start (IGN/START).

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
A full MSFS restart is needed after an update.The systems and FADEC WASM modules and flight_model.cfg / systems.cfg change (new MSFS fuel valves 13 and 14).Restart the sim after installing.

How to use

  1. In an engine fire, follow the ECAM procedure: ENG MASTER OFF, then ENG FIRE pushbutton PUSH. Both close the LP valve.
  2. Watch the LP valve on the SD FUEL page: green inline = open, amber crossline = closed.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A32NX on the ground, engines running at idle. Open the SD FUEL page.Both LP valve symbols green, inline (open).
2Push the ENG 1 FIRE pushbutton. Leave the ENG 1 MASTER switch ON. Start a timer.The ENG 1 LP valve symbol turns to an amber crossline within about 2 s (amber while in transit). No fire warning (no fire). Engine 1 keeps running for now.
3Wait, watching N1 / N2 and the fuel flow of engine 1.Engine 1 runs down by itself after about 1 min 40 s (sea level idle), never later than 2 min 30 s.
4Reset the ENG 1 FIRE pushbutton, set ENG MASTER 1 OFF, then restart the engine with IGN/START and ENG MASTER 1 ON.The LP valve opens again (green inline) and the engine starts normally.
5Set ENG MASTER 2 OFF on a running engine.The ENG 2 LP valve closes on the SD and the engine shuts down as before.

3. A380X: the ENG FIRE pushbutton closes the LP fuel valve

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: Overhead ENG FIRE pushbuttons; SD FUEL page  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-03).

What it is for

A380 FCOM DSC-28-10 ENGINE LP VALVES: "The ENG MASTER sw control the LP valves. The LP valves automatically close, when their associated ENG FIRE pb is pushed." Pushing an ENG FIRE pushbutton closed no valve, so the engine kept running. The four engines now have the same logic as the A32NX, with a 3 s valve travel.

The A380 FCOM gives no shutdown delay. The fuel left after the valve is the 1 gallon tank the FBW fuel model already has between each LP valve and its engine: the engine stops about 17 s after the pushbutton at a sea-level idle (about 24 s at 14 100 ft).

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
ENG FIRE pushbutton pushedThe engine LP valve closes; the engine shuts down after about 17 s at idle; no start or relight while it is starved.
SD FUEL pageLP valve per FCOM DSC-28-20: green inline = open, amber inline = abnormally open, amber crossline = closed.
ENG MASTER switchesUnchanged: they still open and close their engine valves as before.

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
A full MSFS restart is needed after an update.The systems and FADEC WASM modules, flight_model.cfg and systems.cfg change (new feed valves 60 to 63).Restart the sim after installing.

How to use

  1. In an engine fire, follow the ECAM: ENG MASTER OFF, ENG FIRE pushbutton PUSH.
  2. Check the LP valve of that engine on the SD FUEL page.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A380X on the ground, four engines at idle. Open the SD FUEL page.Four LP valves green, inline.
2Push the ENG 1 FIRE pushbutton, ENG MASTER 1 left ON. Start a timer.The ENG 1 LP valve turns to an amber crossline after about 3 s.
3Watch engine 1.It runs down by itself about 17 s after the pushbutton (sea-level idle).
4Try to restart engine 1 with the FIRE pushbutton still pushed.No start: the engine is starved.
5Reset the FIRE pushbutton, ENG MASTER 1 OFF then ON with the start selector.The valve opens (green) and the engine starts.
