1. What it is for

The dual ADR and IR fault procedures ask the crew to move the WXR & TAWS and the XPDR & TCAS to the system that still has valid data. Those lines were hard-coded as done, so the ECAM ticked them even when the wrong system stayed selected. They now follow the selection made on the MFD SURV STATUS & SWITCHING page (PR 5), and the TCAS 1 / 2 FAULT procedures show their XPDR & TCAS line, which was hidden.

4. LINE-UP checklist

The ECAM LINE-UP checklist gets the CABIN CREW line that the flyPad checklist already had (A380 FCOM BEFORE TAKEOFF checklist: CABIN CREW ... ADVISE). It is ticked by the crew.
