1. Standby Navigation Display (SND)

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: Lower ISIS  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-09-29). 10 unit tests.

What it is for

The A380 has two ISIS (integrated standby instrument systems): the upper one is the Standby Flight Display (SFD: attitude, speed, altitude), the lower one the Standby Navigation Display (SND): heading, position and a small navigation of its own, independent of the FMS (A380 FCOM DSC-34-10-20). On the FBW A380 the lower screen was black; it is now the SND.

The waypoint list

The SND has its own list of waypoints, entered by coordinates (it has no database). Press MENU: the menu shows INSERT WPT, INSERT FIX, EDIT WPT / FIX and CLEAR WPT / FIX in cyan; turn the knob to choose, push to select.

  1. MENU, then push on INSERT WPT: the coordinates show, starting from the last waypoint (or your position).
  2. Turn the knob to change the underlined field, push to go to the next one: N/S, degrees, minutes, tenths, then E/W, degrees, minutes, tenths.
  3. Push on the last field: the waypoint is added at the end of the list.

EDIT and CLEAR WPT / FIX: turn to choose the waypoint ("2/3 N5015/E00232", then the FIX last), push to edit or to delete it.

DIR TO and the navigation

  1. Press LS/DIR TO. With a list, turn to choose the waypoint and push. Without a list, enter the coordinates of the point as above.
  2. The navigation starts from your position: FROM, TO (white) and NEXT under the line, the bearing and distance to the TO waypoint top right, and the course on the rose.
  3. Passing abeam the TO waypoint, the SND goes on to the next one.

The FIX

One point you want to watch (a beacon, an airport): MENU, INSERT FIX, enter its coordinates. The SND shows its distance top left, a magenta pointer towards it on the rose, and its coordinates in magenta at the bottom of the list. A new FIX replaces the old one; EDIT / CLEAR WPT / FIX offer it after the waypoints.

How to use

  1. Read heading, track, ground speed and position on the lower ISIS.
  2. Waypoints: MENU, INSERT WPT, set the coordinates with the SET/SEL knob (turn to change, push for the next field).
  3. LS/DIR TO, choose a waypoint and push: the SND guides you there (TO WPT bearing and distance, course, deviation bar).
  4. One point to watch: MENU, INSERT FIX. EDIT / CLEAR WPT / FIX to change the list.
  5. + / - for the brightness.

2. SND power-up self-test

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: Lower ISIS  |  Status: Not sim-tested (2 unit tests). It was installed with the SFD/SND reconfiguration that the user tested on 2026-10-02, with no separate record.

What it is for

The two ISIS of the A380 are the same unit, SFD or SND (A380 FCOM DSC-34-10-20-10). The SFD (upper ISIS) already ran its power-up tests: a yellow INIT box and a countdown for 90 seconds. The SND came on at once. It now behaves as the SFD. The A380 FCOM gives no time: the 90 s and the 10 s below are the ones of the FBW SFD.

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
Cold and dark spawn, first power-upINIT and the seconds left in a yellow box for 90 s; HDG and PPOS flags meanwhile; then the normal SND.
Spawn with the aircraft powered (runway, cruise...)The SND is on at once, no test.
Power lossThe display stays on for 10 s; power back within 10 s: no new test. Longer: off, and the tests run again at the next power-up.
Power supplyAs the SFD: DC ESS bus, or DC HOT 1 above 50 kt.

How to use

  1. Nothing to do: after a cold and dark start, wait for the end of INIT (90 s) before using the SND.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A380X cold and dark. Switch the batteries on (DC ESS powered).The upper (SFD) and lower (SND) ISIS both show INIT with a countdown from 90.
2Wait 90 seconds.The SND shows its rose; HDG / PPOS flags until the ADIRS give heading and position.
3Remove the power for less than 10 seconds, then restore it.The SND stays on; no new INIT.
4Remove the power for more than 10 seconds, then restore it.The SND goes off, then runs INIT again for 90 s.
5Spawn on a runway (powered).The SND is on at once, without INIT.

3. SFD/SND reconfiguration with the MODE pb

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: MODE pb of either ISIS  |  Status: Tested in the sim by the user: OK (2026-10-02). 2 unit tests.

What it is for

A380 FCOM DSC-34-10-20-20-10: the MODE pb "enables to switch from SFD to SND display or to switch off the corresponding standby display". Before, MODE only switched the SND off and on. Now each press goes to the next configuration, and one SFD is always displayed. Both ISIS screens carry both displays; the knob and the pushbuttons of an ISIS act on the display it shows.

What you see

MODE pressedUpper ISIS (1)Lower ISIS (2)
NormalSFDSND
1st pressSNDSFD
2nd pressSFDoff
3rd pressSFDSND (back to normal)
  • The SND waypoint list, DIR TO and FIX are the same whichever ISIS shows the SND.
  • On the SFD, the MENU pb opens the BUGS page (the A380 FCOM puts the bugs in the SFD menu); the A320 ATT RST on that pb is gone (not in the A380 FCOM).

Limitations and why

LimitationWhyWhat to do
No automatic reconfiguration.The FCOM reconfigures automatically on an SFD display failure; FBW has no ISIS display failure.Use the MODE pb.
A full MSFS restart is needed after an update.The cockpit behaviour files and panel.cfg change.Restart the sim after installing.

How to use

  1. Press MODE on either ISIS to go to the next configuration (swapped, SND off, normal).
  2. Use the knob and the pushbuttons of the ISIS that shows the display you want to work on.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A380X powered, both ISIS after INIT. Upper ISIS: SFD, lower ISIS: SND.Normal configuration.
2Press MODE on the lower ISIS.The upper ISIS shows the SND, the lower one the SFD.
3Turn the knob of the upper ISIS (now SND), press its MENU.The SND menu opens on the upper ISIS; the waypoint list is the one entered before.
4Press MENU on the lower ISIS (now SFD).The SFD BUGS page.
5Press MODE again.Upper ISIS SFD, lower ISIS off.
6Press MODE again.Back to normal: upper SFD, lower SND.

4. Normal ISIS configuration at each power-up

Aircraft: A380X  |  Where: Both ISIS  |  Status: Not sim-tested (2 unit tests).

What it is for

The configuration chosen with the MODE pb survived a power cycle: an ISIS could come back swapped or off. The ISIS now start in the normal configuration (upper ISIS SFD, lower ISIS SND) at each power-up. The FCOM (DSC-34-10-20-20-10) says nothing about it: this is a design choice.

What you should see

SituationWhat you should see
ISIS off, then powered (they run INIT)Normal configuration, whatever MODE showed before.
Power loss shorter than the 10 s standbyThe configuration is kept (not a power-up).
Spawn with the aircraft poweredThe configuration is not changed by the spawn.

How to use

  1. Nothing to do: after a power-up the ISIS are back to normal; use MODE again if you need another configuration.

How to test

#Do thisExpected result
1A380X powered, both ISIS on. Press MODE once.Upper ISIS SND, lower ISIS SFD.
2Remove the power for more than 10 seconds (batteries and external power off), then restore it.Both ISIS run INIT; after it, upper SFD and lower SND (normal).
3Press MODE once, then remove the power for less than 10 seconds and restore it.The swapped configuration is kept.
